## The question

After the Hugging Face incident, Sam and Dario support pacing frontier AI. Trump and his government fear that China will use the time to gain. Trump considers nationalization so Washington can set the research schedule.

The dispute is about who can hold a capability stage, what evidence permits it to restart, and whether China accepts comparable checks. Buying a company does not answer those questions.

This is the premise of a fictional meeting, not a report of a joint offer by the CEOs or a real takeover plan. The movement includes many people. Sam and Dario did not jointly found it in this story's source record.

All new laws, votes, acquisitions, settlements, and judgments are assumptions. A funded legislative coalition is not inferred from current seat counts. Continued defiance produces removal only in the authored branch with the specified House and Senate votes.

Full acquisition covers specified domestic businesses and required control rights. It does not acquire unrelated cloud providers, foreign foundries, every subsidiary, or every competing lab. Staff can leave.

The independent testing body survives replacement of executives. A classified schedule does not repeal its powers. In the crisis branch, it clears a narrow planning role; humans choose whether to conduct an operation whose wider effects remain uncertain.

The asset purchase leaves both firms private. A state lab is a separate institution. Defined OpenAI decision rights require legislative and corporate approvals and do not confer ownership of Anthropic. The custody route remains a disputed claim until it is ended; it never becomes lawful ownership through repetition.

## What the fiction adds

The joint October meeting, nationalization proposal, divergent company offers, National AI Act, classified schedule, state lab, and US–China pacing accord are invented.

OpenAI's published standards are not a mandate for government permission before every release. The binding gates in the game arise from new fictional contracts or laws. Sam can favor external scrutiny while negotiating different terms from Dario. Neither CEO alone can promise every required governance vote.

The narrow accord covers declared government-funded programs and specified stages of automated AI research. It requires domestic authority and consent from covered contractors. Other work remains outside it. Washington does not promise a worldwide halt that it cannot deliver. Inspection reduces a defined uncertainty; it does not prove that no hidden program exists.

Future officeholders continue through scene dates unless a branch changes them. Play ends by 2028. The 2035 passages describe later institutions, not a continuing Trump presidency.

## Character use

Public voice evidence remains in the [character briefs](../../../wiki/characters/README.md). Immediate objectives below are writing choices.

- **Trump:** Wants a US lead and an authority he can use. He asks for dates, prices, and who can refuse. A cost can change his offer.
- **Sam:** Acknowledges OpenAI's incident. Wants independent scrutiny and clear conditions to resume work. Qualifies what he can promise for the Foundation and boards.
- **Dario:** Supports US leadership as well as stronger controls. Separates a failed test from an argument about who owns the lab. He is not indifferent to China.
- **Vance:** Turns the demand into votes, funding, decision rights, and a workable institution.
- **Hegseth:** Asks for usable capability on a military schedule. Does not treat a change of owner as a technical fix.
- **Rubio:** Tests reciprocity, uncertainty about China, and what allies will accept.
- **Caine:** States what the assessment supports and what remains a human choice. He is an adviser, not the operational commander.
- **Xi:** Insists on reciprocal obligations. His offer and English dialogue are fiction, not evidence of private intent.
- **Lai, Wei, and Jensen:** Address security commitments or feasible supply within their separate roles. None can promise total control of the network.
- **Counsel and lab director:** Composite fictional roles. They receive the relevant record and speak within it.

Sam and Dario attend negotiations and retention meetings. After replacement, they do not attend classified operational briefings. A departure reaction is a handover scene.

## Persistent choices and convergence

Each run has five decisions. There are 22 decision scenes, 45 reaction scenes, nine ending families, and 48 complete paths.

The state retains ownership, law, capability, agreements, and pacing policy. A shared ending includes the applicable ownership and pacing passages. It cannot silently turn a private compact into public ownership.

Pacing policy records independent gates, classified work under statutory review, supplier or joint review, contract limits, a rushed pilot, or voluntary company commitments. These affect conditional scene text and the ending. They are not another visible numerical score.

The warning convergence retains capability. An understaffed program or rushed prototype produces an unsupported warning in this scenario. A stronger program detects a narrower intrusion that independent sources confirm. Its sponsor remains disputed. This is an authored distinction, not a claim that stronger models cannot make false alarms.

The state lab can reach a shared-capacity agreement after buying tested services. Funding and testing capacity then connect the supply branch back to the pacing dispute.

War requires human authorization, Chinese retaliation, and further decisions to continue. Failure of an inspection does not automatically cause war. Neither a low score nor model output automatically launches a weapon.

## Research and probability

[Rivera et al., Escalation Risks from Language Models in Military and Diplomatic Decision-Making, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03408) reports escalation patterns in simulations with five language models. The checked abstract supports examining that mechanism. It does not predict this President, current systems, or the effect of nationalization.

[Geist and Lohn, RAND, 2018](https://www.rand.org/pubs/perspectives/PE296.html) discusses AI, perception, and nuclear risk. Its publication summary informs the broad concern about advisory systems. The game's alerts, conventional war, and later armistice are original scenario choices.

No source supplies the probability of an ending. Scores are illustrative summaries of oversight, support, capacity, and tension. They do not choose a terminal state. Path counts measure test coverage.
