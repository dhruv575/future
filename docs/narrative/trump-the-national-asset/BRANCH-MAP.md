# Five-level branch map

[Read the script](script.md) · [Author notes](author-notes.md)

Every run has five decisions. Branches can share a later scene, but they retain ownership, legal history, capability, pacing rules, and commitments.

| Level | Scene count | The question changes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 1 | Respond to pacing with public acquisition, custody, or a negotiated deal? |
| 2 | 3 | Accept binding tests, obey an injunction, or choose between company offers? |
| 3 | 6 | Preserve review, impose a classified schedule, build a state lab, or keep a private alternative? |
| 4 | 5 | Seek reciprocal Chinese checks, act with limited evidence, share capacity, rush a pilot, or obey the court? |
| 5 | 7 | Accept inspection, verify a warning, halt a clash, share checkpoints, settle, or risk removal? |

## Four complete paths

Each line lists five actions. These examples are authored outcomes, not likelihood rankings.

1. **Public ownership and a truce:** Seek a law → accept compensated acquisition and binding tests → retain the teams and independent checkpoints → accept the limited pacing accord → permit reciprocal inspections → **An armed truce**. Both domestic lab businesses stay public.

2. **Public ownership and war:** Seek a law → accept full acquisition → replace the leaders and impose a classified schedule → authorize the limited operation → expand after retaliation → **A conventional war**. State ownership does not contain the other side's response.

3. **The failed seizure:** Assert immediate custody → defy the injunction → continue the custody claim → reject the final ruling → contest impeachment while maintaining the order → **A failed seizure**. The authored Senate coalition convicts; Vance succeeds Trump.

4. **A deal with limits:** Offer a deal → accept defined OpenAI rights → leave Anthropic independent → pool capacity → accept shared checkpoints → **The allied system**. Neither lab becomes a wholly state-owned company.

The two L5_WARNING outcomes after independent review depend on earlier capability choices. That state is retained at convergence; the graph does not choose it randomly.
