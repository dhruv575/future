# Donald Trump — Super Intelligence

Implemented coherence revision. The playable source is `branches.json`; `script.md` is its rendered reading copy. The planning notes below describe the design goals, not an additional unimplemented script.

Start in October 2026, after Pacing the Frontier. Donald has three goals:

1. Prevent China from taking the lead in AI.
2. Make AI drive American economic prosperity.
3. Control the direction of AI development and how it is used.

The central question is how to achieve all three when they conflict. Faster deployment can increase output while creating disruption. Keeping the best systems classified can protect an advantage while limiting civilian benefits. Taking control of the labs can increase presidential authority while weakening cooperation or independent review. These are branch possibilities, not automatic consequences.

Nationalization is one tool Donald can choose. It is not his goal or the only subject of the story. Private contracts, public ownership, regulation, and international agreements must each be judged against the three goals.

Keep five decisions per route. Each decision follows a visible event caused by earlier choices. End at the result of the final crisis, without jumping to 2035.

1. **Who controls the labs?** Seek legislation for public ownership, negotiate enforceable terms with private labs, or attempt emergency custody. An order does not automatically transfer lawful ownership or create a functioning research team.
2. **Who can tell the president no?** Set the rules for independent review. On the custody route, first respond to the court and staff who contest the order. Show the operational result in a signed agreement, hearing, or lab scene.
3. **Who gets the economic benefits?** A capable system is ready for a specific civilian use, while broader control questions remain unresolved. Choose how widely American firms can use it, which uses remain restricted, and what happens to workers whose jobs change. Show results through a factory, hospital, business, or household scene. Economic prosperity must be a visible branch outcome, not a passing line about GDP.
4. **What does China's response mean?** A specific incident creates competing explanations. Let intelligence and technical staff show their evidence and uncertainty. Earlier cooperation determines which independent checks are available.
5. **Will Trump accept a limit on his own authority?** Resolve the crisis through verification and a reciprocal deal, a bounded defensive response, or escalation. The same order must have different consequences if earlier choices removed review or lost partner trust.

Possible endings should show concrete results: broad American prosperity with a durable AI lead; a strong economy under private labs that resist presidential control; a public AI effort that delivers civilian benefits; a verified agreement that protects prosperity; a classified lead whose benefits reach few Americans; an American lead used for domestic coercion; China overtaking a divided US effort; loss of model control; or military escalation. These are proposed branches, not forecasts or a final ending count. Each ending must resolve the three goals separately. Winning the race alone is not a complete success.

SpaceX and Meta must exist in this world. Taking control of OpenAI and Anthropic does not give Washington ownership of every US frontier developer. Elon and Mark can dispute the terms or seek different treatment, with any such dialogue clearly fictional.

Before scripting, refresh evidence on legal authorities and the real starting events. Separate established facts, character statements, and scenario inventions. Preserve existing Trump saves under their existing story version when a materially different route is introduced.
