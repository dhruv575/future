## Checks

- The participants can perform or request the proposed actions.
- No one uses information they have not received.
- The disagreement follows from the participants' different needs.
- Each option has a plausible reason to choose it.
- A later scene uses the decision or its consequences.
- Dialogue is invented; any real quote is checked and clearly marked.
- Facts, predictions, and scenario assumptions remain separate.
