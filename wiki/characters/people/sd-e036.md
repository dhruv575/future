## Evidence

**Role.** Japan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs identifies Takaichi as prime minister in her February 2026 AI summit message. Official indexed material identifies her in the same office in June. The latter page did not open. These are dated role observations, not a promise of tenure to 2035. [CH-TW-036](https://www.mofa.go.jp/files/100981462.pdf) [CH-TW-038](https://japan.kantei.go.jp/105/speech/202606/0617naigai.html)

**Public positions.** The full AI message advocates trustworthy systems, interoperability, international cooperation and investment. It presents the Hiroshima AI Process as a way to develop shared practices. It also favors work with countries beyond the richest technology markets. The text is a provisional English translation of a prepared message. [CH-TW-036](https://www.mofa.go.jp/files/100981462.pdf)

The indexed February Diet speech pairs the US alliance with a stated aim of constructive relations with China. A June press-conference extract repeats the China formulation. Both are limited-access evidence. This brief does not reconstruct missing passages or infer that every bilateral dispute was resolved. [CH-TW-037](https://japan.kantei.go.jp/105/statement/202602/20shiseihoshin.html) [CH-TW-038](https://japan.kantei.go.jp/105/speech/202606/0617naigai.html)

**Authority and relationships.** These sources establish a head of government addressing national and international policy. They do not establish the legal conditions for a particular use of force. A game branch that deploys Japanese forces needs a separate review of the applicable process.

## Writer inference

**Voice.** Confidence: low. The full reviewed text is translated, prepared and brief. It is organized around a central value, named institutions and a concrete offer of cooperation. That can guide a public address. It is inadequate evidence for her spontaneous cadence, humor or behavior under challenge. [CH-TW-036](https://www.mofa.go.jp/files/100981462.pdf)

**Plausible wants.** In fiction, she can seek an arrangement that strengthens Japan's technical capacity and public security. Give her a concrete national concern: disrupted shipping, unavailable industrial inputs or unsafe software in essential services. AI should connect to that concern through an event.

**Resistance.** A US request can carry costs for Japanese firms. A technology firm can seek government support while avoiding a domestic obligation. A diplomatic assurance from Beijing may leave a specific operational question unanswered. A scene should make those terms explicit.

**Scene entry.** Use a cabinet-level security or economic decision, or a formal leaders' meeting. A model company's routine evaluation dispute should first go through relevant officials and technical advisers.

**Tension to preserve.** Promoting rapid investment and asking for trusted international practices can both be public aims. The conflict begins when a concrete project cannot meet both on the requested schedule. Do not impose a permanent choice between innovation and safety before the scene establishes one.

**Avoid.** Do not write her as an automatic trigger for war because she has a strong security position. Do not invent a personal AI-weapons doctrine from a general AI summit message. Do not translate institutional Japanese into caricatured English. Any future intervention, resignation or election result belongs to the fiction and needs a visible transition.
