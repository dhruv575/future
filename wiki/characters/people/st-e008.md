## Evidence

**Public role at the source date.** The June 2024 Dwarkesh interview identifies Aschenbrenner as a former OpenAI researcher and discusses a planned investment venture. It does not verify a 2026 job, fund, or portfolio. [CH-RD-017](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/leopold-aschenbrenner)

**Argument.** The essay introduction frames advanced AI as a large industrial and geopolitical change. It emphasizes compute, electricity, capital, and national power. Its future events are the author's expectations. They should not be silently converted into historical facts or the game's default world state. Only the introduction was read for this source. [CH-RD-016](https://situational-awareness.ai/)

**Public reasoning.** He connects scaling and infrastructure to national strategy and government involvement. He also calls for changing beliefs with new evidence. His account of prior employment is not independent corroboration. [CH-RD-017](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/leopold-aschenbrenner)

**Tension.** The same public presentation combines strong directional confidence with dependence on uncertain technical and political assumptions. The writer can use that tension without deciding that he is either infallible or a caricature of overconfidence.

## Writer inference

**Public voice.** The interview uses quantitative steps, repeated conditional projections, and large historical comparisons. He often answers by splitting a broad objection into parts. Use a small amount of this structure. Do not turn every line into a lecture or copy his vocabulary mechanically. [CH-RD-017](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/leopold-aschenbrenner)

**Plausible scene entry.** He could be invited to discuss a capacity plan, advise an investor, or challenge a policy team's estimate of how quickly conditions may change. Give the invitation a concrete purpose and establish the year. Do not assume he retains the same institutional position for a decade.

**Possible wants and resistance.** A fictional version might seek approval for an early investment that becomes expensive or impossible if delayed. Another character could question demand, technical timing, or the political cost. He needs to connect the long-term thesis to a near-term commitment.

**Useful conflict.** Ask what observation would change his recommendation. A strong answer should expose an actual assumption, not make him surrender his whole argument. The player should be able to choose which uncertainty to investigate.

**Avoid.** Do not treat speculative claims about espionage or institutional failures as established conduct by named people. Do not give him private access merely because he once worked at a lab. Do not equate a US-focused strategy with a wish for war.

**Unknowns.** This brief needs a newer source before writing a current financial role, a precise 2026 forecast, or a claim that his 2024 view has remained unchanged. Private loyalties and motives are not established. Any scene set after these sources must show its added fictional assumptions.
