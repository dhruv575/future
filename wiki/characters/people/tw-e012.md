## Evidence

**Role.** KMT chair in April and June 2026 reporting. Party leadership gives her a platform and negotiating access. It is not Taiwan's presidency. [CH-TW-014](https://apnews.com/article/taiwan-opposition-leader-china-trump-13c4c9c7f1bb0b358a4b3df1ceea0bf0) [CH-TW-015](https://apnews.com/article/china-taiwan-cheng-xi-9735f829b2d9d68525ad192253e47fac)

**Public positions.** During her US visit, Cheng argued for dialogue with Beijing and opposed permanent separation as a path to peace. She also said her party supported Taiwan's defense and saw common ground with US efforts to avoid war. Treat that combination as her public case, not a verified guarantee that the policy would work. [CH-TW-014](https://apnews.com/article/taiwan-opposition-leader-china-trump-13c4c9c7f1bb0b358a4b3df1ceea0bf0)

At the April meeting with Xi, she spoke about respect across different political systems and measures to prevent war. AP described both leaders as favoring peaceful reunification while noting that the route remained unclear. [CH-TW-015](https://apnews.com/article/china-taiwan-cheng-xi-9735f829b2d9d68525ad192253e47fac)

A KMT search extract links her peace message to domestic reconciliation and US-China cooperation. The original archive now redirects to a site shell. That item is limited evidence and is not a full transcript. [CH-TW-016](https://www1.kmt.org.tw/english/page.aspx?anum=25943&mnum=112&type=article)

**Institutional limit.** Election results do not erase constitutional offices. Party leadership does not itself confer executive authority. [CH-TW-004](https://english.president.gov.tw/Page/95)

## Writer inference

**Voice.** Confidence: low. The reporting preserves only selected answers and translated remarks. It supports an argument based on avoiding war through engagement. It does not support a precise English speaking rhythm or a private bargaining personality.

**Plausible wants.** A fictional Cheng can seek a visible result from dialogue that she can defend to Taiwan's voters. A trade concession, a restored communication channel or reduced coercion creates a test of her stated approach. She needs a result, not merely a meeting photograph.

**Resistance.** Beijing may want language that is costly at home. US counterparts may demand commitments that weaken her claim of independent engagement. A semiconductor firm may require enforceable terms rather than political assurance.

**Scene entry.** Use a party delegation, a public interview or a domestic negotiation about a proposed agreement. An official state negotiating role must be earned through election or explicit appointment within the fiction.

**Tension to preserve.** A peace offer can be sincere as a public aim while still carrying large political risks. Let another character challenge its terms and enforcement. Do not turn that disagreement into proof of secret betrayal.

**Avoid.** Do not equate her party winning with an automatic TSMC cutoff. If the story uses that flashpoint, write the intervening coalition, legal, commercial and public choices. No reviewed source supplies a personal plan to give Beijing direct control of TSMC. Invented future actions must be marked as fiction.

## Gaps

- Full original-language interviews are needed for voice.
- No sourced plan to cut US access to TSMC was found.
- Party statements do not establish electoral mandate or executive power.
