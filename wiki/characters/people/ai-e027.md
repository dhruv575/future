The fiscal 2025 letter, published in 2026, argues that progress can take several attempts.

### Authority and relationships

They discuss AWS, internal operations, customer products, and partners.
They do not establish personal ownership of every AWS operating decision. [CH-AI-033](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-ceo-andy-jassy-on-generative-ai), [CH-AI-034](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-ceo-andy-jassy-2025-letter-to-shareholders)

**Analysis:** A cloud customer, a model supplier, and Amazon's own application team can need different things.

His investment argument combines long-term patience with pressure for practical value.
Both sides belong in the brief. [CH-AI-033](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-ceo-andy-jassy-on-generative-ai), [CH-AI-034](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-ceo-andy-jassy-2025-letter-to-shareholders)
