### Public positions and changes

In April 2024, he said a prior shortage for recommendation systems shaped a larger GPU purchase.
He presented spare capacity as preparation for opportunities the company could not yet specify.
That is his retrospective explanation. [CH-AI-017](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/mark-zuckerberg), 00:04:22–00:06:47

His July 2024 essay argues that an open AI ecosystem benefits Meta and society.
He connects the business case to avoiding dependence on a closed platform.
He also argues that broad scrutiny can improve safety.
The safety claim is a position, not a settled comparative result. [CH-AI-018](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/07/open-source-ai-is-the-path-forward/)

In September 2026, he announced an enterprise AI business built around models, agents, infrastructure, and existing customer reach. [CH-AI-019](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/launching-meta-enterprise-platform/)
Do not carry the 2024 statement about model-access revenue into 2027 as a permanent business constraint.

### Relationships and tensions

Other developers can be both users of Meta's releases and competitors.
An ecosystem strategy can seek broad participation while serving Meta's commercial position. [CH-AI-018](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/07/open-source-ai-is-the-path-forward/)
The enterprise announcement adds another reason to care about reliability and customer commitments. [CH-AI-019](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/launching-meta-enterprise-platform/)

An argument for openness does not prove that every release will remain open.
