# António Costa

Entity: `SD-E044`; aliases: none. Research checked: 2026-10-11.

[Character research](../README.md) | [Existing dossier](../../research/states-defense/people.md#sd-e044-antonio-costa)

## Evidence

**Role.** President of the European Council. The Council's live role page dates his term from 1 December 2024. It assigns him chairing, continuity, consensus-building and specified external representation. The office has a renewable two-and-a-half-year term. It is not the Commission presidency or a national premiership. [CH-TW-060](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/european-council/president/role/)

**Public positions.** At his handover, Costa emphasized unity across different national histories and interests. He tied European strength to peace, prosperity and a stronger security role alongside transatlantic partnership. [CH-TW-062](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2024/11/29/speech-by-incoming-european-council-president-antonio-costa-at-the-handover-ceremony-with-the-outgoing-president-charles-michel/)

After the July 2025 Beijing summit, he advocated continued talks despite disagreement. He addressed climate cooperation, human rights, Ukraine and trade imbalance. He called for practical follow-through. These remarks describe what he sought and discussed; they do not prove that China changed its conduct. [CH-TW-061](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/07/24/remarks-by-president-antonio-costa-at-the-joint-press-conference-with-president-von-der-leyen-following-the-eu-china-summit-in-beijing/)

**Relationships and limits.** His role requires work with national leaders, the Commission president and the High Representative. The office can help form a common position. It cannot replace the decisions that belong to those other actors. [CH-TW-060](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/european-council/president/role/)

## Writer inference

**Voice.** Confidence: medium for public speeches. The handover text uses parallel examples and repeated collective terms to make diversity a reason for cooperation. The Beijing remarks acknowledge progress, identify disagreement and return to the next negotiation. Use that sequence in a shorter conversational form. [CH-TW-061](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/07/24/remarks-by-president-antonio-costa-at-the-joint-press-conference-with-president-von-der-leyen-following-the-eu-china-summit-in-beijing/) [CH-TW-062](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2024/11/29/speech-by-incoming-european-council-president-antonio-costa-at-the-handover-ceremony-with-the-outgoing-president-charles-michel/)

**Plausible wants.** In fiction, his immediate task can be to find wording or a limited action that several governments will actually support. The objective may be a common incident channel, a shared deadline or a position that keeps a divided summit from failing.

**Resistance.** One member may need trade access; another may demand a stronger security response. A company may mistake a favorable meeting for a binding EU commitment. A foreign leader may try to use differences between members.

**Scene entry.** Use a European Council discussion, a leaders' summit or the preparation of a shared statement. Show which governments disagree and what each must receive. A scene without those differences wastes the distinctive role.

**Tension to preserve.** Unity can make a position more effective while narrowing its scope. A carefully limited agreement may be useful even if it disappoints a company or one member government. He can press for a small binding step instead of a large statement with no support.

**Avoid.** Do not make him a second version of von der Leyen. Do not have him allocate Commission funds or command national forces by himself. Do not turn negotiation into automatic compromise on every principle. A consensus-builder can decide that a proposed formula is unacceptable.

**Possible scene test.** A model incident affects several countries. Sam asks for one EU contact and one permission. Costa can help convene a common response, but the participants disagree about evidence access and cost. The scene ends when they accept a specific next step, not when every disagreement disappears.
