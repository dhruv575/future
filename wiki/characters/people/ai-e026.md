## Evidence

### Role and authority

Nadella writes as Microsoft's chairman and CEO in the 2025 annual report.
The letter describes security, quality, and AI innovation as core priorities.
It places cloud infrastructure and deployed products within the same business plan. [CH-AI-031](https://www.microsoft.com/investor/reports/ar25/index.html)

### Public positions

In February 2025, he argues that AI should show value in the wider economy.
He questions the use of self-declared AGI milestones as the main measure of success.
He connects spending on infrastructure to evidence of customer demand.
His growth examples are criteria for significance, not measured future results. [CH-AI-030](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/satya-nadella), 00:15:18–00:21:39

The annual letter treats reliability and security as essential to global services.
This is a stated management priority.
It does not prove that all services are secure. [CH-AI-031](https://www.microsoft.com/investor/reports/ar25/index.html)

### Relationships and changes

The January 2025 OpenAI agreement described exclusivity in some areas. [CH-AI-004](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2025/01/21/microsoft-and-openai-evolve-partnership-to-drive-the-next-phase-of-ai/)
The April 2026 amendment preserves a primary cloud partnership but allows OpenAI to serve customers across cloud providers.
Microsoft's model and product IP license becomes non-exclusive.
A major shareholding continues.
Use this later agreement when a scene depends on partner freedom. [CH-AI-005](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/04/27/the-next-phase-of-the-microsoft-openai-partnership/)

The relationship supports both cooperation and bargaining.
It does not make Nadella OpenAI's chief executive.
Do not let him order every lab to stop training.
