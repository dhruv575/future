## Evidence

The White House roster identifies Rubio as secretary of state. This brief does not assume any additional concurrent office. [CH-SD-009](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/cabinet/)

At his January 2025 confirmation hearing, Rubio argued that foreign policy should improve US security, strength, and prosperity. He criticized supply-chain dependence and described the Chinese Communist Party as exploiting the global order. These are his stated assessments. [CH-SD-010](https://www.foreign.senate.gov/download/01-15-25-nominations__rubiopdf)

His answers distinguished established Taiwan policy from Beijing's preferred interpretation. He said the president sets foreign policy and the secretary implements it. This makes a difference in scenes: he can negotiate and advise without personally settling every military decision. [CH-SD-010](https://www.foreign.senate.gov/download/01-15-25-nominations__rubiopdf)

The hearing provides direct exchanges with senators. It also contains questions and statements by other people. Those should never be copied into Rubio's position by proximity. [CH-SD-010](https://www.foreign.senate.gov/download/01-15-25-nominations__rubiopdf)

The operational military chain runs through the president, defense secretary, and combatant commanders. The secretary of state is not listed as a combatant commander. [CH-SD-003](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10542)

## Writer inference

**Voice analysis:** Rubio uses historical setup, then a chain of reasons leading to a policy conclusion. In answers, he separates issues and qualifies authority. He can use dry humor under interruption. He does not speak only in threats. [CH-SD-010](https://www.foreign.senate.gov/download/01-15-25-nominations__rubiopdf)

Give him a clear argument with one or two steps. Let him define what a proposed agreement actually means. Avoid making him recite every treaty or teach an entire region's history during a crisis.

**FICTION DESIGN ANALYSIS — scene uses:**

- Put him between a public accusation and the private message needed to keep talks open.
- Give a company a technical conclusion that diplomats cannot safely convert into state attribution.
- Let him ask which government can make the requested commitment.
- Make him distinguish a statement, an agreement, and an enforceable undertaking.
- Give him a limited mandate and a deadline for returning to the president.

An invented objective could be to obtain a verifiable concession while keeping allied support. A useful resistance is an arrangement that shifts costs to the US without a reciprocal benefit. A useful question is whether a counterpart can deliver what it promises.

**Tension to preserve:** A severe assessment of China does not remove the need for diplomacy. His hearing also makes presidential control explicit. That can create friction between a negotiator's preferred terms and the terms he can authorize. [CH-SD-010](https://www.foreign.senate.gov/download/01-15-25-nominations__rubiopdf)
