## Evidence

The live White House page identifies Trump as the 47th US president. Its praise is promotional, not independent evidence. [CH-SD-002](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/donald-j-trump/)

At the July 2025 AI summit, he linked leadership to power supply, permits, infrastructure, and national loyalty. He opposed restrictive state rules. He also acknowledged risks. His stated answer was American leadership, rather than withdrawal from the technology. [CH-SD-001](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202500787/pdf/DCPD-202500787.pdf)

That speech combines competition with bargaining. He praised Xi while promising to keep the US ahead. He addressed Jensen Huang directly. These are public exchanges, not proof of private trust or access. [CH-SD-001](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202500787/pdf/DCPD-202500787.pdf)

The military command chain runs through the president, defense secretary, and combatant commanders. Presidential direction still needs institutional execution. [CH-SD-003](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10542) CRS assigns nuclear-use authorization to the president. It also discusses legal requirements for orders. An adviser does not inherit that authority. [CH-SD-004](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10521)

## Writer inference

**Voice analysis:** The summit speech moves between prepared claims, short verdicts, repeated words, personal anecdotes, and direct audience challenges. Numbers and named people make abstract subjects concrete. He uses praise as well as pressure. This is a public performance sample, not a transcript of private deliberation. [CH-SD-001](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202500787/pdf/DCPD-202500787.pdf)

Use short, active turns. Let him ask for an outcome or a price before an explanation. A brief interruption can change the subject. Use repetition sparingly. Filling every line with superlatives becomes parody.

**FICTION DESIGN ANALYSIS — scene uses:**

- Give him a proposal with a visible gain and a visible concession.
- Make the other character explain who pays, who delivers, and when.
- Let him reject a slow plan, then accept a revised deal with a firm deadline.
- Separate his public announcement from the staff work needed to implement it.
- Give advisers conflicting estimates. He must select among them; he need not solve their technical dispute.

A useful invented objective is a result he can present as a US win. A useful resistance is an open-ended demand without a deliverable. These are scene design choices, not claims about hidden motives.

**Tension to preserve:** National competition and willingness to strike a deal coexist in the same AI speech. Do not flatten him into either a permanent warmonger or an automatic dealmaker. [CH-SD-001](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202500787/pdf/DCPD-202500787.pdf)

## Future assumptions and gaps

For a 2027 scene, state the office assumption and the fictional crisis date. Do not carry presidential powers through 2035 without a separate political premise. A meeting with a company founder needs an invitation, staff channel, and reason. Public familiarity does not establish unrestricted access.

Voice confidence is high for public rhetoric. It is lower for private exchanges. Do not use invented first-person dialogue as evidence.
