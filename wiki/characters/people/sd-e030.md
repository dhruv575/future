## Evidence

Hegseth identified Paparo as Indo-Pacific commander at the May 2025 Shangri-La Dialogue. This is dated role evidence. It does not establish continued tenure through 2035. [CH-SD-008](https://www.iiss.org/globalassets/media-library---content--migration/files/shangri-la-dialogue/2025/transcripts-final/p1/sld2025_firstplenarysession_pete-hegseth_as-delivered.pdf)

At his February 2024 nomination hearing, Paparo linked allies, resources, and deterrence. He supported helping Ukraine while acknowledging pressure on some precision-weapon inventories. He described staffing and command arrangements in concrete terms. [CH-SD-013](https://www.armed-services.senate.gov/download/transcript-24-02_---02-01-24)

On Taiwan, he supported stronger defenses. He placed the scale of US assistance with the president and Congress. Those answers preserve a difference between a commander's assessment and a national political commitment. [CH-SD-013](https://www.armed-services.senate.gov/download/transcript-24-02_---02-01-24)

In November 2024, an AP account attributed more direct stockpile concerns to him. He called for replenishment. Only the indexed report was accessible in this pass. This supports a dated change in emphasis, not a claim that he contradicted himself without cause. [CH-SD-014](https://apnews.com/article/28564bbed21f72b9a3c6b3cd9c086bc7)

A combatant commander operates under the president and defense secretary. Theater command does not give control over allied governments. [CH-SD-003](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10542)

## Writer inference

**Voice analysis:** The hearing shows short initial answers followed by operational detail. He names a requirement or constraint and distinguishes present arrangements from improvements. He can make a firm recommendation without claiming authority over policy. [CH-SD-013](https://www.armed-services.senate.gov/download/transcript-24-02_---02-01-24)

Give him quantities, availability, timing, or a named assumption. Do not make each line an acronym. One concrete constraint can explain why a seemingly simple order is hard.

**FICTION DESIGN ANALYSIS — scene uses:**

- Put two theaters in competition for the same scarce capability.
- Have him explain what a new AI tool can change and what physical shortage remains.
- Make a supplier's delivery schedule collide with an exercise or readiness deadline.
- Let an ally control an access decision his plan requires.
- Force the briefing to distinguish a desirable option from an option available now.

A useful invented objective is to keep a credible response available. A useful resistance is a promise that cannot survive deployment conditions. A plausible question asks what the force can do if the network fails.

**Tension to preserve:** Support for partners and concern about inventory can coexist. A later scene should show the inventory change that explains a harder recommendation. Do not treat every caution as opposition to helping an ally. [CH-SD-013](https://www.armed-services.senate.gov/download/transcript-24-02_---02-01-24) [CH-SD-014](https://apnews.com/article/28564bbed21f72b9a3c6b3cd9c086bc7)

## Future assumptions and gaps

A future storyline must establish his job and available forces. Neither a public warning nor a readiness requirement proves that war will occur on a specific date.

The corpus contains an indexed 2026 military-law speech, but its body could not be verified here. It is not used as a voice foundation. Voice confidence is medium for formal military advice.
