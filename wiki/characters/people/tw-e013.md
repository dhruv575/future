## Evidence

**Role.** The reviewed TPP page names Huang as party chair and head of its policy and strategy department. Its experience list also calls him an incumbent legislator. This brief does not treat that undated field as a verified current legislative seat. [CH-TW-017](https://www.tpp.org.tw/en/leaders-modal.php?mid=61)

**Public positions.** In a February 2024 party account of a meeting with Bonnie Glaser's delegation, Huang supported closer US defense cooperation, stronger defense spending and renewed cross-strait dialogue. He also emphasized legislative, judicial, tax and housing reform. The report attributes some separate remarks to Huang Shan-shan; those must not be reassigned to him. [CH-TW-018](https://www.tpp.org.tw/en/events-detail.php?id=42)

A 2025 party extract describes Huang and then-KMT chair Eric Chu jointly addressing tariffs and judicial issues. The same text makes accusations against the DPP. Those allegations establish the party's rhetoric, not their truth. The full page returned an access error. [CH-TW-019](https://www.tpp.org.tw/en/events-detail.php?id=95)

**Authority and relationships.** A party chair can bargain and organize. The constitutional executive offices retain their own duties. [CH-TW-004](https://english.president.gov.tw/Page/95)

## Writer inference

**Voice.** Confidence: low. The full material reviewed is a party summary, not sustained speech. Do not derive a courtroom manner from his legal degree alone. The published agenda supports questions about process, evidence and accountability. It does not establish a fixed emotional register.

**Plausible wants.** In a fictional crisis, he can seek disclosure of what the executive promised a foreign AI firm and who authorized it. He can demand a review mechanism that survives the immediate emergency. This gives him a specific task and a reason to oppose a rushed decision.

**Resistance.** Government officials may invoke secrecy. A company may use commercial confidentiality. Other opposition partners may prefer a quick deal. A useful scene forces him to choose which information is essential and what delay the public can bear.

**Scene entry.** Bring him in through party negotiations, public scrutiny or a committee role that the story has actually assigned. A party meeting and a state security briefing have different attendance rules.

**Tension to preserve.** Cooperation with the KMT on one question need not mean agreement on every foreign-policy question. His stated support for defense cooperation and for dialogue also should not be compressed into a single pro-US or pro-Beijing label.

**Avoid.** Do not make opposition equivalent to sabotage. Do not have him command a foundry, promise a treaty or reveal classified logs without a cause and consequence. If a future story gives him executive office, state the transition and research its powers. AI model release preferences and personal views on machine autonomy remain gaps.
