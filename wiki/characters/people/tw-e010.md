## Evidence

**Role.** Premier of Taiwan on the live Executive Yuan profile. The career list dates that office from 2024 and lists previous cabinet, party and legislative work. This establishes institutional experience, not temperament. [CH-TW-008](https://english.ey.gov.tw/Page/61C21F458EFF8175)

**Public positions.** A May 2026 cabinet readout attributes support for an AI education program to Cho. The program emphasized teaching capacity, student literacy and responsible use. It was a proposed administrative program, not evidence that all schools had gained these capabilities. [CH-TW-009](https://english.ey.gov.tw/Page/61BF20C3E89B856/6d38ee32-ecd7-4e59-94f1-74acecf52354)

An October 2026 readout records his request for a supplementary energy budget after pressure on oil and gas supplies. He linked subsidies to household prices and reliable supply. The government sought legislative passage before further administration. This is useful evidence of an implementation problem with a deadline and another institution's consent. [CH-TW-010](https://english.ey.gov.tw/Page/61BF20C3E89B856/579db15d-c082-43fb-ad9d-0d4380a54083)

**Authority and relationships.** The Executive Yuan answers to the legislature. Cho must secure budget authority as well as carry out Lai's political direction. [CH-TW-004](https://english.president.gov.tw/Page/95)

## Writer inference

**Voice.** Confidence: low. The reviewed material is an official biography and edited accounts of remarks. It supports subject matter and stated priorities. It does not support claims that he is personally terse, conciliatory, angry or cautious. Start with a restrained professional voice. Obtain a full press conference before making verbal habits distinctive.

**Plausible wants.** In a fictional AI crisis, give him an executable plan: which ministry leads, what it costs, which services continue, and when the legislature must act. He can care about a model incident because it interrupts payments, energy operations or public confidence.

**Resistance.** A chief executive may promise a technical fix without a service-restoration date. A security official may demand restrictions that ministries cannot apply uniformly. Opposition legislators may want evidence and a narrower budget. These are substantive constraints rather than obstacles inserted to make him look weak.

**Scene entry.** Use a cabinet coordination meeting or a budget negotiation. Sam should arrive through a minister or a documented request. Cho need not be the first person to receive raw model logs.

**Tension to preserve.** An emergency measure can help one industry while shifting costs to households or other firms. Fast action can also create a promise that the government cannot fund. Give the premier a reason to ask for a smaller, deliverable first step.

**Avoid.** Do not make him an automatic voice of presidential agreement. Do not let a cabinet announcement cause instant physical capacity. Separate approval, appropriation, contracting and delivery in the scene's causal sequence. No reviewed source establishes a specific personal policy on autonomous weapons.
