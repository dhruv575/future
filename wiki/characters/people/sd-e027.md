## Evidence

The current White House roster lists Hegseth under Secretary of War. The May 2025 speech identifies him as secretary of defense. Preserve the date and title used by each source. At Shangri-La, he stressed readiness, deterrence, and allied contributions. He said his task was to preserve choices for Trump. [CH-SD-008](https://www.iiss.org/globalassets/media-library---content--migration/files/shangri-la-dialogue/2025/transcripts-final/p1/sld2025_firstplenarysession_pete-hegseth_as-delivered.pdf)

He warned about China while stating a wish to avoid war. He urged faster delivery of autonomous systems. These are policy statements, not proof of capability or a forecast of war. [CH-SD-008](https://www.iiss.org/globalassets/media-library---content--migration/files/shangri-la-dialogue/2025/transcripts-final/p1/sld2025_firstplenarysession_pete-hegseth_as-delivered.pdf)

He publicly named Caine and Paparo as partners in producing options. That supports institutional interaction. It does not establish agreement on every private decision. [CH-SD-008](https://www.iiss.org/globalassets/media-library---content--migration/files/shangri-la-dialogue/2025/transcripts-final/p1/sld2025_firstplenarysession_pete-hegseth_as-delivered.pdf)

The defense secretary sits between the president and combatant commanders in the operational chain. The chairman has a different advisory role. [CH-SD-003](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10542)

## Writer inference

**Voice analysis:** His speech uses forceful declarations, repetition, and contrasts. He credits Trump often. He asks the audience to show commitment through action. [CH-SD-008](https://www.iiss.org/globalassets/media-library---content--migration/files/shangri-la-dialogue/2025/transcripts-final/p1/sld2025_firstplenarysession_pete-hegseth_as-delivered.pdf)

Do not make him a quiet standards reviewer. Let him press for a usable answer. Keep most lines short. A forceful demand can coexist with a concrete question about readiness.

**FICTION DESIGN ANALYSIS — scene uses:**

- Put him between a deployment deadline and a commander's unresolved concern.
- Let him ask what capability a delay costs.
- Make the supplier distinguish a working demonstration from a fieldable system.
- Give him an allied contribution to negotiate, rather than treating allies as extra US units.
- Require a staff decision, budget step, or presidential instruction before a large policy becomes action.

A useful invented objective is to produce a credible military option on time. A useful resistance is a vague promise of future performance. An invented concession could be a limited trial with a clear stopping condition. These are dramatic options, not predictions of his decisions.

**Tension to preserve:** Strong threats and stated war avoidance appear together in the source. They should create a difficult negotiation, not an automatic order to attack.
