## Evidence

The June 2026 PRC meeting record identifies Kim as WPK general secretary and president of State Affairs. It describes talks with Xi through party and state channels. This is China's official account, not independent proof of either leader's private commitments. [CH-SD-017](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/mfa_eng/xw/zyxw/202606/t20260609_11940480.html)

His January 2024 policy speech, preserved by NCNK, addresses industry, regional living conditions, science, education, and defense. It criticizes administrative obstacles and demands implementation. The same speech rejects the previous reunification framework with South Korea and emphasizes military preparation. [CH-SD-030](https://ncnk.org/node/2517)

A November 2024 weapons-exhibition speech says earlier US negotiations confirmed hostility. Only an indexed passage was accessible here. It is evidence of the published message, not proof that future negotiation is impossible. [CH-SD-031](https://kcnawatch.org/newstream/1732281036-492754456/speech-the-respected-comrade-kim-jong-un-made-at-the-opening-ceremony-of-the-military-hardware-exhibition-defence-development-2024/)

The source set shows a leader speaking through party and state institutions. It does not establish a transparent internal command process or a technical AI decision rule. [CH-SD-017](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/mfa_eng/xw/zyxw/202606/t20260609_11940480.html) [CH-SD-030](https://ncnk.org/node/2517)

## Writer inference

**Voice analysis:** The official English translation uses formal address, directives, sharp criticism, and shifts between national welfare and defense. It combines administrative complaints with claims of national resolve. This is curated translated text, not his natural English voice. [CH-SD-030](https://ncnk.org/node/2517)

Avoid comic accents, random outbursts, or a constant desire to launch weapons. Use deliberate instructions and a specific concern. Allow a subordinate to report a concrete production or supply problem.

**FICTION DESIGN ANALYSIS — scene uses:**

- Give him a weapons claim whose production schedule does not match the demonstration.
- Make an external offer useful but conditional.
- Put a domestic target beside a competing security demand.
- Let officials bring conflicting reports about a technical failure.
- Require an explicit choice about what can be shown publicly and what needs further work.

A useful invented objective is to secure a capability that preserves freedom of action. A useful resistance is a bargain that places key decisions with another state. A plausible question concerns whether a promised system can be produced, maintained, and used reliably.

**Tension to preserve:** The 2024 speech includes development demands and hard security language. Reducing the character to nuclear threats erases documented parts of the public agenda. [CH-SD-030](https://ncnk.org/node/2517) His later message about failed US talks should be dated, not converted into a permanent refusal to speak. [CH-SD-031](https://kcnawatch.org/newstream/1732281036-492754456/speech-the-respected-comrade-kim-jong-un-made-at-the-opening-ceremony-of-the-military-hardware-exhibition-defence-development-2024/)

## Future assumptions and gaps

Voice confidence is low. A Korean-language review is needed before detailed dialogue. Avoid treating the official English translator's wording as a personal habit.

No personal policy on frontier models was verified. Do not infer one from general support for science or weapons development.

China–DPRK contact does not make Kim an instrument controlled by Xi. Future cooperation, office, and crisis choices require their own sourced basis or explicit fictional assumptions.
