## Evidence

He tied cooperation to agreements between national leaders. [CH-SD-023](https://www.xinhuanet.com/politics/20240912/2c2a117fd6064ecf8b889c785175c267/c.html)

A January 2026 defense-ministry notice announced an investigation of Zhang and Liu Zhenli. A September 2026 notice announced their expulsion from the Party and military and referral of suspected offenses. Direct access failed; indexed official text was available. These are official disciplinary announcements, not independent findings about private motives. [CH-SD-025](https://www.mod.gov.cn/gfbw/qwfb/16439106.html) [CH-SD-024](https://www.mod.gov.cn/gfbw/qwfb/16488072.html)
