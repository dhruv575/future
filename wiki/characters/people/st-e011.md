## Evidence

**Role and relationships.** Hendrycks's personal site identifies him as executive director of the Center for AI Safety and an adviser to xAI and Scale AI. It records a Berkeley PhD and technical work on benchmarks and robustness. This is a self-published live biography. The scope of each advisory role is not specified. An adviser is not automatically a company's decision-maker. [CH-RD-023](https://danhendrycks.com/)

**Joint policy work.** He coauthored Superintelligence Strategy with Eric Schmidt and Alexandr Wang. Its abstract combines deterrence, nonproliferation, and competitiveness, including a proposed regime based on preventing destabilizing bids for AI dominance. This is a joint policy argument, not official doctrine or proof that the proposed system would be stable. [CH-RD-024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05628)

**Limits.** Only the paper's abstract was reviewed for this brief. Coauthorship does not reveal which passages each person wrote, where they disagreed, or how Hendrycks speaks. Do not infer a personal willingness to conduct a particular attack from a strategic framework.

## Writer inference

**Voice confidence: low.** The source set is sufficient to place him in the cast as a safety researcher with industry advisory links. It is insufficient for distinctive personal dialogue. Use professional, technical phrasing and make clear when he is reporting a result, offering advice, or discussing a joint paper.

**Plausible scene entry.** An evaluation review or a policy discussion is a credible fictional setting. Specify whether he appears for CAIS, as an individual, or under an advisory engagement. Those roles can have different information and obligations.

**Possible wants and resistance.** A useful scene can center on whether an evaluation supports a public claim. The obstacle could be limited access or incompatible definitions of the tested capability. Avoid giving him universal authority to declare a model safe.

**Tensions to investigate.** Safety research, commercial advice, and strategic competition can create different institutional demands. That is a possible writing problem, not evidence of misconduct, insincerity, or an actual conflict in a particular decision.

**Avoid and unknowns.** Do not collapse his view into Schmidt's or Wang's. Do not infer a private motive from an affiliation. Before writing a major speaking role, obtain a full individual interview and a dated account of his authority in the relevant organization.
