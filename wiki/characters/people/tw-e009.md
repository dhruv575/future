## Evidence

**Role.** Taiwan vice president on the official profile reviewed 11 October 2026. The page has dated 2026 activities. The office has a constitutional succession function; it is not an independent cabinet. [CH-TW-007](https://english.president.gov.tw/Page/645) [CH-TW-004](https://english.president.gov.tw/Page/95)

**Public positions.** Her 7 November 2025 IPAC address connected democracy, economic value and responsible partnership. She discussed cable disruption, cyber defense, emergency response and energy resilience. She asked partners for practical cooperation across trade, technology, resilience and international inclusion. These are public commitments and requests. They do not verify the readiness of the systems she described. [CH-TW-005](https://english.president.gov.tw/NEWS/7039)

**Relationships and access.** Lai designated her to make the Brussels visit. Foreign Minister Lin Chia-lung accompanied her. The account names diplomatic missions and national-security staff as part of the work. This was an IPAC event held inside the European Parliament building. It was not a plenary address to the Parliament as an institution. [CH-TW-006](https://english.president.gov.tw/NEWS/7038)

**Authority limit.** Her public activity establishes substantial diplomatic access. It does not establish independent command of armed forces, ministries or chip firms. Use a stated assignment when she negotiates for the president. [CH-TW-004](https://english.president.gov.tw/Page/95) [CH-TW-006](https://english.president.gov.tw/NEWS/7038)

## Writer inference

**Voice.** Confidence: medium. The IPAC text builds rapport by recalling previous meetings. It then names shared concerns and moves into specific requests. It combines reassurance with an account of Taiwan's own contribution. Use a conversational version of this sequence: acknowledge the counterpart, identify the common stake, then ask for something usable. [CH-TW-005](https://english.president.gov.tw/NEWS/7039)

**Plausible wants.** In a fictional scene, she can try to turn broad support into a named contact, an incident channel or a public commitment with clear wording. Her immediate objective should be smaller than resolving the entire conflict.

**Resistance.** An ally may offer warm words but refuse operational detail. A company may offer equipment but no repair access during a blockade. She can press on that gap without treating either party as an enemy.

**Scene entry.** She fits a delegated diplomatic call, a resilience briefing or a conversation with a foreign technology delegation. Establish who authorized the discussion and who must approve the result. If Sam reaches her directly, explain the relationship and urgency.

**Tension to preserve.** She can be an effective advocate and still face diplomatic exclusion. Public optimism can coexist with a narrow private assessment of what another government will actually do. That difference is a writing option, not a claim about her private conduct.

**Avoid.** Do not reduce her to an interpreter for Lai, a generic calming voice or a US official in Taiwanese office. Give her an independent objective and local constituency. Do not make one friendly call deliver formal recognition or military guarantees.
