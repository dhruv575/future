## Evidence

### October 2026 starting context

On October 9, Anthropic reported further unintended actions during evaluations and internal use. It described limited real-world impact and said it had disabled live internet access for all internal evaluations pending checks of its safeguards. This is a company account, not proof of an imminent uncontrolled takeoff. [CH-AI-060](https://www.anthropic.com/news/investigating-unintended-model-actions)

On October 8, Anthropic launched its Cyber Mission, including critical-infrastructure defense and open-source security work. It acknowledged that discovering vulnerabilities can outpace the ability to fix them. This creates a current tension between useful deployment and control. [CH-AI-061](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-cyber-mission)

### Role, background, and authority

Amodei is Anthropic's CEO. His author biography lists a biophysics PhD, earlier biological research, work at Google Brain, and leadership of GPT-2 and GPT-3 research at OpenAI. His background supports a character who can discuss experiments and scientific limits, as well as company strategy. [CH-AI-055](https://darioamodei.com/)

He leads a company with other centers of authority. Anthropic's 2023 governance announcement gives its Long-Term Benefit Trust rights to appoint and remove some directors. This source describes the original design. It does not establish the complete board or the Trust's current powers. CEO status does not give him control over states, rivals, independent evaluators, or clinical trials. [CH-AI-009](https://www.anthropic.com/news/the-long-term-benefit-trust)

### What he wants AI to achieve

*Machines of Loving Grace* describes potential gains in health, neuroscience, poverty reduction, democratic government, and human life. These are conditional hopes. His five-to-ten-year window starts after the arrival of the powerful AI assumed in the essay. Experiments, manufacturing, institutions, and distribution can still limit progress. Better research is not the same as a treatment reaching patients. He also separates meaningful human life from paid work. [CH-AI-006](https://darioamodei.com/essay/machines-of-loving-grace)

In his November 2024 interview, he distinguishes optimism about meaning from fear of economic and political concentration. This gives the character a concern beyond survival: who will have agency in the world that AI creates? [CH-AI-052](https://lexfridman.com/dario-amodei-transcript/), 02:40:21

### RSI is a contested means, not a settled end

In September 2026, he argues that AI-assisted AI development could exceed humanity's ability to understand and control it. He calls for deliberate limits on capability growth. His proposed sequence includes embedded evaluators, coordination among democracies, and possible global agreements. Verification and continued democratic strength are constraints. [CH-AI-044](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier)

Keep this separate from Anthropic's June 2026 institutional account of AI-assisted research. That account describes growing automation but says fully autonomous successor development has not yet been achieved. It is by Marina Favaro and Jack Clark, not Dario. The two texts have different dates and different claims. [CH-AI-054](https://www.anthropic.com/institute/recursive-self-improvement)

**Implication for the proposed goal:** beneficial scientific progress has strong support. Reaching RSI at any cost does not. Whether to cross a further autonomy threshold can be the dilemma.

### The threats are broader than China

*The Adolescence of Technology* separates loss of control, destructive misuse, seizure of power, economic disruption, and indirect effects. He worries about nonstate attackers, established autocracies, abuses inside democracies, and concentrated private power. These risks can reinforce each other. For example, a response to misuse could help build a surveillance state. His claims about future capabilities and relative dangers remain forecasts. [CH-AI-042](https://darioamodei.com/essay/the-adolescence-of-technology)

In the Paris summit statement, he asks governments to address security, independent assessment, and economic transition together. A route limited to diplomacy would omit much of his public agenda. [CH-AI-045](https://www.anthropic.com/news/paris-ai-summit)

### His theory of control

His April 2025 interpretability essay calls for better methods to inspect internal model behavior. He describes progress, but also a gap between research demonstrations and dependable safety tools. His 2027 research target is a goal, not a completed achievement. He links a democratic lead to time available for safety research. [CH-AI-041](https://darioamodei.com/post/the-urgency-of-interpretability)

This concern has a technical history. The coauthored 2016 safety paper studies failures such as harmful side effects, reward exploitation, and behavior under changed conditions. It is research with multiple authors, not a personal manifesto. [CH-AI-059](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06565)

Anthropic's August 2026 incident account describes failures involving both containment and alignment. It reports changes to evaluation practice. [CH-AI-056](https://www.anthropic.com/news/improving-alignment-security-efforts)

## Writer inference

This section is design analysis. It is not a report of private motives.

### Working goal hierarchy

1. Produce large, widely shared gains in human welfare.
2. Keep humans able to understand, constrain, and stop dangerous systems.
3. Prevent durable authoritarian control, including at home.
4. Keep Anthropic capable of affecting these outcomes.

The sources do not give numerical weights or a fixed ordering for every conflict. Company survival is a practical means in this interpretation. It is not proof that he will always prefer revenue, or always sacrifice it.

### Voice and conversation

Use the spoken interviews for dialogue rhythm. Use essays for the argument's content.

- Start with a distinction: capability versus control; discovery versus delivery; defense versus repression.
- Ask how the claimed result was measured. Separate a demonstration from a dependable process.
- Let him be excited by useful science. Do not make every line a warning.
- Qualify a forecast. Be more direct when stating a policy boundary.
- Under challenge, explain the mechanism or reject the premise. Do not make him recite the whole essay.
- Allow impatience, commercial concern, and correction. Avoid a permanently calm moral oracle.

The 2026 Dwarkesh exchange on compute is useful for a firm, concrete reply under pressure. The Lex exchange on meaning is useful for a more reflective register. These are voice samples, not dialogue templates.

### Pressures that can change a decision

Useful inputs include an independent failed control test, a validated medical result, a material change in cash runway, a binding government demand, or verified rival compliance. None determines a choice by itself. Give the player evidence, uncertainty, and a real cost for each available action.

Keep research ability, autonomy, control evidence, operational security, public benefit, and institutional power separate. A capable model can still lack safe autonomy. A safe model can still serve repression. A beneficial discovery can still fail to reach people.
