This brief separates public evidence from writing guidance. Future roles, private scenes, and dialogue are fiction unless separately sourced.

## Evidence

**A material role change.** Scale's announcement on 12 June 2025 says Wang would join Meta and remain on Scale's board. It appoints Jason Droege as interim CEO. A scene after this date must not casually describe Wang as Scale's continuing CEO. The announcement does not specify his later Meta title or all his decision rights. [CH-RD-027](https://scale.com/blog/scale-ai-announces-next-phase-of-company-evolution)

**Public advocacy before the move.** His January 2025 letter to President Trump argues for US leadership in AI. It calls for investment, workforce development, federal adoption, energy, and safety measures that do not block innovation. It also offers Scale as a partner. This is both a policy argument and a corporate public letter. [CH-RD-028](https://scale.com/blog/win-the-ai-war)

**Joint research relationship.** Wang coauthored Superintelligence Strategy with Dan Hendrycks and Eric Schmidt. The paper offers a framework for national competition and risk management. This proves a public collaboration, not that the three have identical views or share authority in each other's organizations. [CH-RD-024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05628)

**Tension.** The chronology matters. Advocacy as a data-company CEO precedes a move to a major AI developer. That transition can alter what a character is responsible for. It does not by itself establish that his beliefs changed, or that a past public statement was insincere.

## Writer inference

**Voice confidence: low for conversation.** The public letter uses a direct competitive frame and turns it into a short list of actions. It addresses an audience with political power and offers a practical partnership. This is a useful guide to prepared advocacy, not a reliable sample of private speech. [CH-RD-028](https://scale.com/blog/win-the-ai-war)

**Plausible scene entry.** Before the move, a government adoption or evaluation discussion can fit his role at Scale. After it, establish the relevant Meta role with a newer source before giving him power over a model release. A Scale board seat is not equivalent to running daily operations.

**Possible wants and resistance.** A fictional version might seek a specific commitment: funding, access to a government use case, or agreement on how capability is evaluated. The opposing participant could question data rights, reliability, or institutional dependence.

**Useful conflict.** Make the disagreement about who can deliver the proposed capability and who will be accountable for it. His experience around evaluation and adoption should create a different scene function from a frontier-model scientist.

**Avoid.** The metaphor of an “AI war” does not establish a wish for armed conflict. Do not turn a US-leadership argument into an order to attack China. Do not retain an obsolete title because it makes a scene easier to write.

**Unknowns.** An extended individual interview and a current Meta role source are needed for a major speaking part. This record does not establish personal ties to the president, private investment motives, or the terms of confidential company decisions.
