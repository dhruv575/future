## Evidence

A reproduced defense-ministry readout identifies Liu as CMC member and Joint Staff Department chief in December 2023. It records a video call with US chairman Charles Q. Brown. [CH-SD-026](https://www.xinhuanet.com/mil/2023-12/22/c_1212318865.htm)

The account says Liu supported renewed military communication on a basis of equality and respect. He linked a stable relationship to US treatment of China's stated core interests. He repeated the PRC position on Taiwan and maritime disputes. These are attributed positions, not this brief's legal judgments. [CH-SD-026](https://www.xinhuanet.com/mil/2023-12/22/c_1212318865.htm)

The January 2026 defense-ministry notice announced an investigation. Its September 2026 notice announced expulsion from the Party and military and referral of suspected offenses. Only indexed official text was accessible. The announcement does not independently establish the underlying allegations. [CH-SD-025](https://www.mod.gov.cn/gfbw/qwfb/16439106.html) [CH-SD-024](https://www.mod.gov.cn/gfbw/qwfb/16488072.html)

The constitution assigns armed-forces leadership to the CMC. A historical chief-of-staff title should not be treated as independent authority to choose war. [CH-SD-033](https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/law%C3%82%C2%A0%C3%A2%E2%82%AC%E2%80%B9sregulations/201911/20/content_WS5ed8856ec6d0b3f0e9499913.html)
