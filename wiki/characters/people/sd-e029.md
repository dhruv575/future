## Evidence

The indexed Joint Chiefs biography identifies Caine as chairman from April 11, 2025. The direct page was blocked. Treat this as official indexed role evidence, not full-page verification. [CH-SD-012](https://www.jcs.mil/Leadership/Article-View/Article/1974872/gen-dan-caine/)

In his confirmation hearing, he acknowledged that his nomination was unusual. He promised candid military advice, including advice leaders might not welcome. He accepted that the chairman advises rather than commands. [CH-SD-011](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-119shrg63038/pdf/CHRG-119shrg63038.pdf)

He declined to speculate beyond his nomination-stage knowledge. He moved sensitive capability discussion toward a closed session. He supported credible nuclear deterrence and providing options to the president. These answers show distinctions between advice, disclosure, and decision authority. [CH-SD-011](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-119shrg63038/pdf/CHRG-119shrg63038.pdf)

CRS explains that the chairman transmits orders but does not command combatant forces. Its nuclear primer also separates presidential authorization from military advice. [CH-SD-003](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10542) [CH-SD-004](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10521)

## Writer inference

**Voice analysis:** His hearing replies are courteous and bounded. He often answers the narrow question, identifies a limit, and offers a later discussion. He occasionally uses humor, including a historical military reference when defending candid advice. [CH-SD-011](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-119shrg63038/pdf/CHRG-119shrg63038.pdf)

This is not a license to write evasive boilerplate. Give him a specific uncertainty and a useful next step. An honest lack of information should change the decision, not merely decorate the dialogue.

**FICTION DESIGN ANALYSIS — scene uses:**

- Ask him to compare two military options with different risks.
- Make him identify which assumption could invalidate the preferred option.
- Let him resist pressure to give a precise answer unsupported by available evidence.
- Place the technical expert and operational commander in the same briefing.
- Have him carry a recommendation to civilian leaders while preserving the commander's distinct responsibility.

A useful invented objective is a decision based on a realistic account of force readiness and risk. A useful resistance is a presentation that hides uncertainty behind a single confidence score. A plausible question concerns what has changed since the previous assessment.

His relationship with a president should be represented through appointments, advice, and lawful direction. The fact of appointment does not prove personal loyalty in every dispute. Nor does a promise of candor prove that all later advice was candid.

**Tension to preserve:** He can advocate better military capability and still insist that another person makes the political decision. Do not turn him into either a passive messenger or an independent commander of every force. [CH-SD-011](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-119shrg63038/pdf/CHRG-119shrg63038.pdf) [CH-SD-003](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10542)

## Future assumptions and gaps

A 2030 or 2035 scene must establish whether he remains chairman. Do not use a present biography as proof of future tenure.

The record here does not show his private view of a named AI company or model. Such views need explicit fictional setup.

Voice confidence is medium for formal briefings. It is low for private exchanges under combat pressure.
