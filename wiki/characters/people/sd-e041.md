## Writer inference

**Voice.** Confidence: medium. The prepared speeches combine historical examples, short moral assertions and a practical appeal to collective action. The US reception speech also uses light audience-specific humor before discussing serious differences. Use the structure selectively; a crisis meeting need not sound like a commemorative address. [CH-TW-051](https://www.government.nl/documents/2026/07/02/speech-by-prime-minister-rob-jetten-at-the-independence-day-reception-the-hague) [CH-TW-052](https://www.government.nl/documents/2026/09/24/speech-by-pm-rob-jetten-at-the-united-nations-general-assembly-2026)

**Plausible wants.** In fiction, he can seek a coalition position that protects Dutch security, employment and industrial capability while retaining useful alliances. Make the immediate task a specific policy decision with named responsibilities.

**Resistance.** A US official may ask for a restriction on short notice. ASML may ask for predictability and a clear scope. Coalition partners may disagree about the cost or legal basis. Jetten must find a plan his cabinet can own.

**Scene entry.** A major export-policy change, a European Council meeting or a national infrastructure decision fits. Sam should not receive a personal promise that substitutes for a licensing process. The relevant minister and officials must exist in the chain.

**Tension to preserve.** Defending international rules can create disagreements with an ally. Supporting a national technology firm does not mean accepting every commercial request. An agreement on a principle can still leave implementation contested.

**Avoid.** Do not make the Dutch prime minister ASML's manager. Do not treat a European Council intervention as a unilateral EU decision. Do not extrapolate a climate-policy background into a specific view on nuclear war or model capabilities.

**Possible scene test.** The player seeks a temporary equipment-service exception during an AI infrastructure crisis. Jetten asks what makes it temporary, who can verify the end-use, and what happens if the exception fails. Those questions are proposed fiction grounded in the institutional setting, not an actual conversation.

**Date note.** The UN page's body says 25 September 2026; its URL contains 24 September. The ledger uses the body date.
