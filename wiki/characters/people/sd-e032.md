## Evidence

The State Council roster, updated August 2026, lists Wang as foreign minister. His March 2025 press conference also identifies him as a Politburo member. [CH-SD-018](https://english.www.gov.cn/statecouncil/202008/12/content_WS5f334b75c6d029c1c26379c3.html) [CH-SD-019](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/web/wjbz_673089/zyjh_673099/202503/t20250307_11570443.shtml)

In the 2025 conference, he rejected the idea that the US could suppress China while keeping a good relationship. He also argued that the two states must coexist. On DeepSeek, he presented external restrictions as obstacles that could spur Chinese innovation. These are his attributed positions. [CH-SD-019](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/web/wjbz_673089/zyjh_673099/202503/t20250307_11570443.shtml)

In March 2024, he argued for useful, safe, and fair AI. He called for human control, risk assessment, and a UN-centered international body. He opposed technology restrictions framed as exclusion. His position includes governance as well as access. [CH-SD-020](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/wjdt_674879/wjbxw_674885/202403/t20240307_11255225.shtml)

He explicitly presents policies through Xi's initiatives in these answers. That supports a scene role as an advocate and negotiator for state policy. It does not establish permission to alter every commitment. [CH-SD-019](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/web/wjbz_673089/zyjh_673099/202503/t20250307_11570443.shtml) [CH-SD-020](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/wjdt_674879/wjbxw_674885/202403/t20240307_11255225.shtml)

The constitution assigns armed-forces leadership to the CMC. Foreign-minister status alone does not establish military command. [CH-SD-033](https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/law%C3%82%C2%A0%C3%A2%E2%82%AC%E2%80%B9sregulations/201911/20/content_WS5ed8856ec6d0b3f0e9499913.html)

## Writer inference

**Voice analysis:** His press answers often reject a premise, define a principle, and state a preferred response. He uses balanced phrases, rhetorical contrasts, historical references, and occasional metaphor. He can be pointed without sounding rushed. [CH-SD-019](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/web/wjbz_673089/zyjh_673099/202503/t20250307_11570443.shtml)

Write controlled replies that change the terms of the question. Keep the core request clear. Do not use long lists of official phrases unless the scene is specifically about a public statement.

**FICTION DESIGN ANALYSIS — scene uses:**

- Place him in a negotiation where both sides want an incident channel but dispute what it implies.
- Give him a technical proposal linked to export restrictions.
- Let him separate a private clarification from a public concession.
- Make him ask whether the other side speaks for its government or only a company.
- Let a useful agreement fail over scope, representation, or implementation rather than simple hostility.

A useful invented objective is to secure a reciprocal process that preserves China's stated position. A useful resistance is a unilateral demand packaged as neutral administration. A plausible question asks who will receive the evidence and what they can do with it.

**Tension to preserve:** His support for AI governance and opposition to exclusion are both documented. A request for common testing need not produce automatic agreement on who sets the standard. [CH-SD-019](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/web/wjbz_673089/zyjh_673099/202503/t20250307_11570443.shtml) [CH-SD-020](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/wjdt_674879/wjbxw_674885/202403/t20240307_11255225.shtml)

## Future assumptions and gaps

Voice confidence is medium. The Chinese originals support rhetorical structure. They do not establish an English-speaking conversational habit. English dialogue should be marked as an adaptation.

Do not infer hidden motives from an official phrase. For future scenes, establish his office, instructions, and the channel through which he can obtain approval. An elegant reply should not by itself change policy.
