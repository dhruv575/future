[Character index](../README.md) · [Existing dossier](../../research/states-defense/people.md#sd-e031-xi-jinping)

## Evidence

A September 2026 Xinhua event record identifies Xi as CPC general secretary, PRC president, and CMC chairman. [CH-SD-034](https://www.news.cn/politics/leaders/20260930/00e3694ccd3340df8c1afd293a42bb74/c.html) The constitution places leadership of the armed forces with the CMC and overall responsibility with its chairman. [CH-SD-033](https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/law%C3%82%C2%A0%C3%A2%E2%82%AC%E2%80%B9sregulations/201911/20/content_WS5ed8856ec6d0b3f0e9499913.html)

In April 2025, a Politburo study-session readout linked AI self-reliance to foundational research, chips, software, industry, and applications. It also called for regulation, monitoring, early warning, and emergency response. Competition and control both appear in the stated program. [CH-SD-015](https://www.xinhuanet.com/politics/leaders/20250426/63f5cde8f4b54e22ba35aa7ec7884b3a/c.html)

His November 2023 San Francisco speech used personal memories, shared wartime history, and a partner-or-rival framing. It urged cooperation while demanding respect for China's development path. These claims express his position; they do not establish that all Chinese conduct follows them. [CH-SD-016](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/web/gjhdq_676201/gj_676203/bmz_679954/1206_680528/1209_680538/202311/t20231116_11181541.shtml)

A November 2024 official account says Xi and Biden endorsed human control over nuclear-use decisions. The text records a political statement, not a verification system. [CH-SD-032](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202411/17/content_WS67394d2ac6d0868f4e8ed13c.html)

The June 2026 Kim meeting record stresses top-level guidance and party relations. It shows a leadership channel with the DPRK. It does not put Kim under Chinese command. [CH-SD-017](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/mfa_eng/xw/zyxw/202606/t20260609_11940480.html)

## Writer inference

**Voice analysis:** The San Francisco speech is structured and patient. It uses historical stories, parallel clauses, and broad principles before specific offers. The AI readout is directive: identify weaknesses, set priorities, assign institutional work. Audience and setting matter. [CH-SD-016](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/web/gjhdq_676201/gj_676203/bmz_679954/1206_680528/1209_680538/202311/t20231116_11181541.shtml) [CH-SD-015](https://www.xinhuanet.com/politics/leaders/20250426/63f5cde8f4b54e22ba35aa7ec7884b3a/c.html)

Use deliberate, complete statements. Let a question test whether a subordinate understands the political objective. Avoid making every sentence a proverb. Do not turn translated official language into a comic accent.

**FICTION DESIGN ANALYSIS — scene uses:**

- Give him competing reports on economic cost, technical dependence, and security.
- Make a subordinate propose a limited compromise that appears to affect a wider principle.
- Let him demand an implementation plan, not merely a demonstration.
- Use officials to deliver technical detail. He chooses priorities and acceptable tradeoffs.
- Give a foreign offer a disputed meaning, so wording and public presentation matter.

A useful invented objective is to retain control over a strategic dependency while avoiding a damaging crisis. A useful resistance is an arrangement that makes essential capacity dependent on another state. Neither objective should be presented as private testimony.

**Tension to preserve:** Cooperation abroad and self-reliance at home can coexist. Likewise, support for human nuclear control does not settle every issue of military automation. [CH-SD-015](https://www.xinhuanet.com/politics/leaders/20250426/63f5cde8f4b54e22ba35aa7ec7884b3a/c.html) [CH-SD-032](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202411/17/content_WS67394d2ac6d0868f4e8ed13c.html)

## Future assumptions and gaps

State his office and the command structure at each future date. High authority does not give perfect information or automatic implementation.

Voice confidence is medium. The public record is rich, but speeches are curated and translations affect English rhythm. Private exchanges, internal rivalries, and crisis thresholds remain unknown. A scenario must label any invented version of them.
