## Evidence

The August 2026 State Council roster lists Dong as minister of national defense. [CH-SD-018](https://english.www.gov.cn/statecouncil/202008/12/content_WS5f334b75c6d029c1c26379c3.html)

The official Chinese readout of his June 2024 Shangri-La appearance emphasizes regional cooperation and firm defense of claimed core interests. Its indexed body was available, but direct access failed. It is a summary, not a verbatim speech. [CH-SD-021](https://www.mod.gov.cn/gfbw/qwfb/16313255.html)

The event host's report gives more detail. Dong defended China's stated no-first-use nuclear policy. He rejected technology blockades and strongly opposed Taiwan independence. During questions, he returned to sovereignty and challenged descriptions of Chinese maritime conduct. These are attributed claims, not this brief's legal conclusions. [CH-SD-022](https://www.iiss.org/globalassets/global-content---content--migration/dialogues/sld/sld2024/iiss-shangri-la-dialogue-2024-report.pdf)

The same report records that he engaged with questions about Taiwan and the Philippines. This supports a diplomatic and public-representation function. It does not prove that he personally controlled the operations discussed. [CH-SD-022](https://www.iiss.org/globalassets/global-content---content--migration/dialogues/sld/sld2024/iiss-shangri-la-dialogue-2024-report.pdf)

The PRC constitution assigns armed-forces leadership to the CMC. Do not import the US defense secretary's command role into Dong's title. [CH-SD-033](https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/law%C3%82%C2%A0%C3%A2%E2%82%AC%E2%80%B9sregulations/201911/20/content_WS5ed8856ec6d0b3f0e9499913.html)
