## Evidence

An April 2026 Kremlin transcript, republished through PublicNow, identifies Putin as president. Only the indexed English opening was accessible. It links AI development to state sovereignty and describes increasingly capable models. The rest of that meeting was not verified. [CH-SD-028](https://ebs.publicnow.com/view/427494A487B3276DF44B82CCC129E70875AD5E18)

A Russian-language mirror of his November 2025 AI Journey speech contains a long indexed transcript. It emphasizes domestic models, energy, computing capacity, and practical adoption. It also discusses cooperation with international partners. The direct mirror and original Kremlin body were inaccessible. Treat this as a provisional speech lead, not a fully authenticated transcript. [CH-SD-027](https://prezident.org/tekst/stenogramma-konferencii-puteshestvie-v-mir-iskusstvennogo-intellekta-19-11-2025.html)

The Russian constitution gives the president direction of foreign policy and the commander-in-chief role. Its text describes formal powers. It does not establish the private process for any specific military decision. [CH-SD-029](https://www.minjust.gov.ru/ru/documents/8011/)

## Writer inference

**Provisional voice analysis:** The indexed 2025 speech moves from a formal introduction to instructions for officials. It connects technical capacity to sovereignty, then asks for results rather than reports. The opening includes an exchange with German Gref about the event's length. This suggests more range than uninterrupted menace, but provenance limits confidence. [CH-SD-027](https://prezident.org/tekst/stenogramma-konferencii-puteshestvie-v-mir-iskusstvennogo-intellekta-19-11-2025.html)

For now, use a reserved, deliberate register. Let him make the speaker define a dependency or commitment. Do not reproduce distinctive mannerisms from an unverified mirror.

**FICTION DESIGN ANALYSIS — scene uses:**

- Give him an offer that improves capability but creates an external dependency.
- Make a domestic supplier explain the difference between a target and a working system.
- Let advisers disagree about whether a partnership reduces isolation or increases dependence.
- Give a diplomatic concession a separate military and industrial cost.
- Require a concrete next step after a claim of technological progress.

A useful invented objective is to gain useful capacity while retaining decision authority. A useful resistance is an arrangement that exposes critical data or makes delivery dependent on a foreign actor. These are scenario proposals. They are not recovered private motives.

**Tension to test, not assume:** The provisional speech links technological sovereignty with international cooperation. A partnership could be attractive and still raise concern about control. [CH-SD-027](https://prezident.org/tekst/stenogramma-konferencii-puteshestvie-v-mir-iskusstvennogo-intellekta-19-11-2025.html)

Do not write Russia as an automatic extension of China's decisions. Any coordinated action needs an explicit agreement or clearly marked fictional premise.

## Future assumptions and gaps

Voice confidence is low because primary transcript bodies were blocked. Before writing long direct dialogue, obtain the official Russian transcript or authenticated recording and compare it with the mirror.

This brief does not predict nuclear use, secret bargains, or loyalty between leaders. It also does not verify claims that domestic AI products match foreign systems.

Office, health, capability, and partnerships through 2035 require separate assumptions. Use indirect reporting when evidence cannot support a detailed personal exchange.
