## Evidence

**Role and authority.** The AI Futures Project's live team page identifies Kokotajlo as executive director. It records prior governance research at OpenAI and work on scenario planning. It names Eli Lifland and other colleagues. This establishes an outside research organization and former employment, not continuing authority inside OpenAI. [CH-RD-012](https://ai-2027.com/about)

**Scenario work.** AI 2027 is a narrative forecast with explicit assumptions and alternative branches. It uses fictional firms. Its title must not be treated as every author's fixed median date or as evidence that the depicted chain of events will occur. The project's later clarifications matter. [CH-RD-013](https://ai-2027.com/)

**Later public position.** In an interview published in August 2026, Kokotajlo discusses a proposed negotiated slowdown with China. He separates what he recommends from what he expects. He also says he changed his mind about sequence: effective US domestic regulation should precede a wider deal. He acknowledges political obstacles and remaining failure modes. [CH-RD-014](https://80000hours.org/podcast/episodes/daniel-kokotajlo-ai-2040-plan-a/)

**Tension.** This gives the writer more than a prophet of imminent disaster. He argues for preparation while expecting real implementation to be incomplete. His preferred plan is not a claim that governments have adopted it. Nor does former employment validate an allegation about a later company's private conduct.

## Writer inference

**Public voice.** The 2026 transcript alternates short answers and conditional explanations. He accepts some objections and corrects others. Preserve that range. Confidence is medium because the main sample is one edited interview. [CH-RD-014](https://80000hours.org/podcast/episodes/daniel-kokotajlo-ai-2040-plan-a/)

**Plausible scene entry.** Use an invited forecast review, policy workshop, or public exchange. A former employer's executive can receive his published criticism without meeting him in person. A private meeting requires a fictional reason and an explicit account of who arranged it.

**Possible wants and resistance.** He could seek an agreement to act before a visible disaster makes action obvious. The resistance could be a demand for stronger proof, concern about a rival's compliance, or a belief that the proposed pause would concentrate power.

**Useful conflict.** Make someone ask which part of his proposal is essential and which part could change. Give him room to revise a means while defending the objective. The interview's distinction between prediction and recommendation supports this approach. [CH-RD-014](https://80000hours.org/podcast/episodes/daniel-kokotajlo-ai-2040-plan-a/)

**Avoid.** Do not make his function merely to announce a year of doom. Do not import his probabilities as the game's objective odds. Do not turn a claimed private disagreement into settled fact or assume that all his coauthors agree with every proposal.

**Unknowns.** The record does not reveal private motives, present access to OpenAI systems, or how he would react to a specific fictional crisis. Any later organizational role or personal reconciliation must be authored as fiction.
