## Evidence

**Role and expertise.** RAND's live biography identifies Moon as a mathematician. It describes work on AI, emerging technology, cybersecurity, supply chains, and workforce problems. It records earlier postdoctoral work in mathematical physics. This establishes a technical research background, not an operational role in cyber activity or military command. [CH-RD-030](https://www.rand.org/about/people/m/moon_alvin.html)

**Research relationship.** He coauthored Can AGI Rivals Hold Back? with Tobias Sytsma in September 2026. The accessible summary describes a model linking domestic oversight, verification, and incentives for restraint. Its results depend on assumptions about actors and their choices. They are not a probability estimate for a real crisis. [CH-RD-031](https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA5163-1.html)

**Evidence limit.** This review read the publication summary, not the full mathematical report. It can identify the research question and collaboration. It cannot settle the technical robustness of every result, identify each author's contribution, or establish Moon's personal policy position.

## Writer inference

**Voice confidence: low.** There is no basis here for a distinctive personal speaking style. Use careful role-based dialogue only. Do not invent mannerisms from a mathematics background or assume emotional distance.

**Plausible scene entry.** An evaluation of a proposed monitoring system is a credible fictional setting. He can be asked whether the scheme provides the information that a policy model assumes it provides. Establish who invited him and the scope of the review.

**Possible wants and resistance.** A useful immediate goal is to make an assumption explicit before officials rely on the conclusion. The resistance can be a deadline or a demand that a conditional result be simplified.

**Useful conflict.** Separate the mathematical condition from the institution needed to satisfy it. Another character should be able to argue that the condition is too expensive, too intrusive, or politically unavailable. The scene can then produce a real choice.

**Avoid.** Do not place Sytsma's podcast language in Moon's mouth. Do not make him approve a policy merely because he analyzed it. Do not treat a mathematical model as access to a rival's intentions.

**Unknowns.** An individual interview or authored talk is needed before extensive personal dialogue. Current policy preferences, private relationships, speaking cadence, and reactions under pressure remain unverified. Use a fictional technical adviser if a scene needs details this record cannot support.
