## Evidence

**Role.** President of the Legislative Yuan on its current profile. It identifies him as KMT and gives 1 February 2024 as the start date. This is a parliamentary office, separate from the presidency and premiership. [CH-TW-011](https://www.ly.gov.tw/Pages/Detail.aspx?nodeid=120&pid=1)

**Public positions.** Before a May 2026 European trip, Han described Taiwan's limited diplomatic space and promoted its strengths in chips, healthcare, business and democracy. The delegation included members of the DPP, KMT and TPP. It provides a concrete example of him representing a plural legislature abroad. [CH-TW-012](https://focustaiwan.tw/politics/202605100005)

In a 2019 Harvard appearance, while mayor of Kaohsiung, he attributed his election success to dissatisfaction with government. The PTS report contains translated extracts that foreground voters and delivery. It is historical evidence from a different role. It cannot by itself define how he chairs the legislature now. [CH-TW-013](https://news.pts.org.tw/article/428705)

**Authority and limits.** The speaker does not control every legislator's vote. Budgets require legislative action. [CH-TW-004](https://english.president.gov.tw/Page/95) [CH-TW-011](https://www.ly.gov.tw/Pages/Detail.aspx?nodeid=120&pid=1)

## Writer inference

**Voice.** Confidence: low. The available spoken material is selected reporting, partly translated. It suggests accessible comparisons and direct appeals to ordinary citizens in public remarks. It does not justify a comic persona, constant slogans or invented shouting in a closed meeting. [CH-TW-012](https://focustaiwan.tw/politics/202605100005) [CH-TW-013](https://news.pts.org.tw/article/428705)

**Plausible wants.** In fiction, he can seek an agreement that lets the legislature act without appearing to surrender its scrutiny. He can ask the executive to defend a requested power, put a deadline on it, or explain who bears its cost.

**Resistance.** The executive may frame delay as danger. Other lawmakers may see that frame as pressure to approve a poorly specified bill. His problem is to keep a workable process while members have different incentives.

**Scene entry.** Use an emergency legislative consultation, a meeting with caucus leaders, or a parliamentary delegation. If he speaks with Sam, the topic should concern evidence lawmakers need or a promise made to Taiwan, not secret tactical command.

**Tension to preserve.** His party identity and his duty to represent the legislature can pull in different directions. A successful foreign visit does not erase domestic disputes. Conversely, domestic opposition does not prove hostility to every defense or technology measure.

**Avoid.** Do not treat KMT membership as a grant of control from Beijing. Do not let him order TSMC to cut off a customer by personal instruction. An electoral victory changes offices and bargaining power; it does not remove the rest of Taiwan's institutions. His exact private views on frontier AI remain unverified.
