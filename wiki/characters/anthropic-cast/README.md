The profiles separate evidence from writer inference. Dialogue is fictional. Role pages alone do not establish a person's voice or private beliefs. Daniela, Brian, and Krishna have low voice confidence in this pass. Their dialogue uses plain language tied to their work.

The cast has overlapping concerns. Jared can question a claimed gain. Jan can support useful research automation. Chris can be excited by an incomplete discovery. Daniela can protect a research team. A disagreement in the game is a scenario choice, not a reported personal dispute.

The story assumes continued involvement after the opening. After closure, an adviser may help with the transition. This does not imply continued employment by an operating Anthropic.

Edit [cast.json](cast.json), then run `python wiki/characters/anthropic-cast/build.py`, `python wiki/tools/build_indexes.py`, and `python wiki/tools/build_characters.py` from the repository root.
