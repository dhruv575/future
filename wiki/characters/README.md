## What each brief contains

- Dated roles, authority, and limits.
- Public positions and relevant changes.
- Documented relationships and points of tension.
- Voice guidance tied to actual speech or writing.
- Possible reasons to enter a scene, make a request, or resist it.
- Gaps, source links, and access limits.

**Evidence** describes the public record.
**Writer inference** proposes ways to use that record in fiction.
Neither a proposed goal nor an invented private exchange is a sourced fact.

Voice confidence describes the material available for writing.
It does not measure honesty, competence, or the probability of an action.

| Confidence | Use |
| --- | --- |
| High | Extended direct material supports the sampled public register. Private speech still requires inference. |
| Medium | Useful material exists, but its range, translation, or setting limits the voice guidance. |
| Low | Material is thin, filtered, or mainly institutional. Use restrained dialogue and research further before a large speaking role. |

Confidence remains specific to the brief. A delivered speech and a live interview offer different evidence.
Read the gaps even when the label is high.

## Scope and dates

The main wiki has a 10 October 2026 cutoff.
This collection is a separate research extension checked on 11 October 2026.
Older records remain available for their source context.

A live biography can confirm a role at access without supplying a publication date.
A dated speech can establish a position at that date without confirming the speaker's current office.
Refresh both before writing a later scene.

Several briefs preserve historical roles or record a role change.
Do not restore an old title merely because an existing sprite or entity card uses it.
Any office, appointment, relationship, or event in 2027–2035 needs an explicit scenario assumption.

The writing uses short sentences and plain terms.
It has not had a full ASD-STE100 dictionary audit.
