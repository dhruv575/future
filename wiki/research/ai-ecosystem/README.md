## Use rules

A model is not automatically a weapon. An agent is a deployed system. An operator can use that system for ordinary work, defense or attack.

Keep these evidence classes separate: controlled test, vendor allegation, reported incident, external review, public proposal and scenario analysis.

The record called "confidence" concerns support for that claim. It is not the chance that a future event will occur.

Authority, incentives and game choices in entity cards are analysis unless a field says otherwise. They do not describe private beliefs.

No reviewed source gives a calibrated probability of AI causing US-China war by 2035. Branch probabilities remain unset.

Prose uses short, direct sentences inspired by STE. This is not a claim of certified ASD-STE100 compliance.

Machine-readable arrays are in [data/parts/ai-ecosystem](../../data/parts/ai-ecosystem/). The AI prefix is local to this packet. Link government and military authority to the wider corpus before play.
