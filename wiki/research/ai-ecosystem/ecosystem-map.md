Do not freeze the map at older product names. A release inventory and a company inventory serve different purposes.

Cloud relationships are not always exclusive. Investment also does not imply direct command over every model decision.

Separate a capacity target from available compute. Record operational date, power supply, chip type, access terms and workload allocation before using a number.

The game should treat a model copy as a distinct asset. Record its owner, host, license and safeguards. A model developer does not necessarily control an independent copy.

Available weights do not mean the complete training process is public. The DeepSeek repository also lists special conditions for derived model families. [AI-S019](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1)

This packet gives model families stable IDs. It does not assert that a listed historical release is the latest model or the best model.

See the wider corpus for fabs, chip equipment, power constraints, governments and military command.
