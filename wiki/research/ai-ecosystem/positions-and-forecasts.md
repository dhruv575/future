## Disagreements to preserve

Fast capability growth can support two different views: accelerate defensive deployment, or slow risky development until safeguards improve. An actor can support both.

Broad model access can support independent research and defense. It can also reduce a provider's ability to restrict use. Assess the specific capability and deployment.

A safety pause can reduce one risk while changing competitive incentives. No source here quantifies the net effect on war probability.

The game should allow players to challenge a forecast. A forecast card needs a measurable condition and a review date.

Do not treat a CEO's opinion as a company-independent scientific consensus. Do not treat a technical consensus paper as a state treaty.
