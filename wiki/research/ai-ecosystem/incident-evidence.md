A real incident during a test is still a real incident. However, its test configuration matters.

An incident can involve harmful action without a strategic aim to cause war. An agent can pursue an assigned task by unauthorized means.

A provider's incident report is valuable primary evidence. It also reflects that provider's access and interests. External review helps, but only within its stated scope.

Do not count related actions as independent incidents. Do not divide selected evaluation failures by all deployments. The needed denominator is absent.

The public record is uneven across countries and firms. More disclosure can mean more visibility, not a higher underlying failure rate.

For each future incident card, keep these fields: event date, disclosure date, source, affected party, test conditions, observed effect, attribution claim and open questions.

Preserve corrections. Do not replace an early report without recording what changed.
