# A human-directed cyber operation crosses an escalation threshold

Record: AI-X002. Status: hypothesis. Horizon: 2026-2035.

Trigger: Human operators use an AI agent for a limited objective; effects or attribution create a wider crisis.

This dossier is original scenario analysis. Sources establish components, not the full causal chain. No branch has an assigned probability.

The playable roles include a provider security lead, lab chief and incident responder. A state operator can be added from the government corpus. Keep its strategic objective explicit. The AI does not need an independent geopolitical aim for this branch to matter.

Anthropic's 2025 account is the key precedent: it describes human target selection and extensive automated work using a US provider's agent. That is evidence against a simple country-to-model ownership map. See [AI-S001](https://www.anthropic.com/news/disrupting-AI-espionage).

Use separate fields for task delegation and strategic authorization. A human can authorize a broad task while failing to authorize a specific harmful effect. The game should permit negligence, misunderstanding and deliberate abuse as different hidden explanations.

Impact should be observable. Record data access, service interruption and verified physical effects separately. Unconfirmed damage reports should be evidence cards with their own reliability.

A provider may restrict service access. A downloaded model has a different control path. Do not let one account ban remove all AI capability from an adversary.

A defensive branch can improve patching, detection and recovery. It may still impose costs. The game should track system downtime, operator workload and false alerts instead of assuming that a defensive agent has perfect judgment.

Termination needs confirmation. Stopping the original human tasking is not automatically proof that every delegated process has stopped. This is a design condition to investigate, not a finding that all present systems persist after shutdown.

## Branch choices

- **Suspend suspicious hosted access and share verified indicators.** Condition: The provider identifies relevant accounts and has useful logs. Result: One access route closes, but other operators or models may remain available.
- **Treat all use of a foreign model as proof of foreign state command.** Condition: Decision makers collapse model origin into attribution. Result: The wrong state or company may receive blame.
- **Separate espionage, disruption and physical damage in the assessment.** Condition: Responders can determine observed effects. Result: The response can match the evidence and preserve restraint options.
- **Continue a limited operation after adverse effects emerge.** Condition: The actual human operator controls further tasking. Result: The operation can exceed political intent and become harder to terminate.

## Limits

- Anthropic's 2025 attribution remains a vendor assessment.
- The 80-90 percent task-work estimate is not a probability of operation success or proof of full independence.
- No operational attack steps are part of this dossier.
- Espionage and armed conflict are not identical states.
