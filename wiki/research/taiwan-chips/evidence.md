# Evidence ledger: Taiwan and chips

Research cutoff: 2026-10-10. Scenario horizon: 2035.

Each entry has a type and an evidence date. Confidence refers to the claim as written. It does not state a war probability. A reported claim can be well documented even when its predicted effect is uncertain.

# Source access and limits

The full source register is [sources.json](../../data/parts/taiwan-chips/sources.json). It records the exact access level. "Full text" means that a substantive body was accessible and relevant passages were read. It does not mean every page of a long filing was audited. "Partial" means a summary or subset was read.

Use English legal translations with care. The reviewed cross-Strait statute shows a 2022 amendment date. Recheck the controlling Chinese text and later law before coding a legal decision rule. Several company sources describe plans. Do not convert plans into current production.
