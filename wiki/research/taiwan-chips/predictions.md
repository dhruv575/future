# Forecasts and conditional findings

No event probability is assigned in this module. The studies use different units: company plans, expert responses, economic exposure and scenario outcomes. These units cannot be pooled into a single war probability.

## TW-P001

**forecast.** US aggregate fab capacity could grow 203% from 2022 to 2032 and reach 14% of global capacity.

Issued: 2024-05-08. Horizon: 2032.

- Conditions: Investment plans and policy support proceed.
- Limits: Publisher summary reviewed; industry-sponsored forecast; Not leading-edge AI capacity and not a war forecast.

[TW-S030: Semiconductor Industry Association / Boston Consulting Group](https://www.semiconductors.org/policies/supply-chain-security/)

## TW-P002

**conditional_result.** A favorable Taiwan election result could prompt Beijing to improve relations.

Issued: 2024-01-22. Horizon: The survey's 2024 election scenario.

- Conditions: Beijing sees the winner as favorable.
- Limits: Over 80% describes expert agreement, not event probability; Do not copy this rate into 2028 or 2032.

[TW-S033: CSIS ChinaPower](https://chinapower.csis.org/surveying-experts-us-and-taiwan-views-china-approach-taiwan-2024/)

## TW-P003

**scenario.** Commercial compliance could give a quarantine political effect without a complete trade stop.

Issued: 2024-06-05. Horizon: Unspecified crisis.

- Conditions: Beijing attempts a quarantine; Carriers and customers comply.
- Limits: Hypothetical mechanism; enforcement and reaction vary; The source's terminology does not settle legality.

[TW-S031: CSIS ChinaPower](https://features.csis.org/chinapower/china-quarantine-taiwan/)

## TW-P004

**warning.** A blockade could cause severe hardship and uncontrolled escalation.

Issued: 2025-07-31. Horizon: Unspecified future blockade.

- Conditions: Beijing imposes a blockade; Opposing actors resist or widen action.
- Limits: Publisher abstract only; Wargame outcomes are not frequencies in the real world.

[TW-S034: CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/lights-out-wargaming-chinese-blockade-taiwan)

## TW-P005

**conditional_result.** A full Taiwan trade stop would expose well over $2 trillion in annualized economic activity.

Issued: 2022-12-14. Horizon: At the start of the study's blockade scenario.

- Conditions: All Taiwan trade stops.
- Limits: Activity at risk, not GDP loss; 2022 economic structure; no sanctions or second-order effects.

[TW-S035: Rhodium Group](https://rhg.com/research/taiwan-economic-disruptions/)

## TW-P006

**conditional_result.** Broad China sanctions would produce large spillovers and coalition coordination problems.

Issued: 2023-06-22. Horizon: A major Taiwan crisis.

- Conditions: G7 considers broad sanctions.
- Limits: No fixed sanctions package is assumed in this corpus; Costs can motivate restraint as well as escalation.

[TW-S036: Rhodium Group / Atlantic Council](https://rhg.com/research/sanctioning-china-in-a-taiwan-crisis-scenarios-and-risks/)
