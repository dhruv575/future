# TW-X001: Election to chip cutoff

Status: hypothesis. Window: a future election through 2035. No probability assigned.

A party that favors closer ties with Beijing wins office. A US chip cutoff does not follow from that fact alone. KMT leaders have publicly supported dialogue and Taiwan's defense. This is evidence against treating engagement as proof of a cutoff policy. [TW-C007; AP, 2026-06-12](https://apnews.com/article/taiwan-opposition-leader-china-trump-13c4c9c7f1bb0b358a4b3df1ceea0bf0).

## Required causal steps

| Step | Required condition | Failure branch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Win office | A candidate wins the relevant election. | The party stays outside executive office. |
| 2. Choose policy | Leaders prefer a specified chip restriction. | Dialogue proceeds with normal trade. |
| 3. Obtain authority | The measure has a legal route, or actors break existing law. | Review, veto or public resistance stops it. |
| 4. Execute | Agencies and firms can implement the measure. | Compliance differs by company or jurisdiction. |
| 5. Reduce usable US supply | Orders, inventories and alternate plants do not offset the restriction. | New deliveries decline only in some products. |
| 6. Change strategic beliefs | US leaders see a serious loss of future AI capability. | They accept delay or find substitutes. |
| 7. Choose escalation | Leaders reject available economic and diplomatic responses. | Talks, relief or rationing contain the dispute. |

Steps 2 and 5-7 are model assumptions. They require separate evidence.

A covered cross-Strait political agreement faces Article 5-3 review and referendum rules. A constitutional or territory change faces different constitutional rules. Do not apply those referendum rules to every export action. The legal basis for a future selective chip restriction remains unresolved. [TW-C002–003, TW-C053; Constitution](https://english.president.gov.tw/Page/95); [Cross-Strait Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/ENG/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=Q0010001).

TSMC also has its own board and officers. A cabinet change is not a board election. [TW-C012; TSMC governance](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/board-of-directors).

## Player choices

A president can seek dialogue, propose a measure, or seek legislative support. A legislator can support, amend or oppose it. A TSMC leader can seek legal review, propose continuity terms, or change a lawful investment plan.

Record the exact product, destination, legal instrument, duration and exemptions. Keep overseas subsidiaries and customer contracts separate.

## Restraint and counterevidence

The expert survey found that a result seen as favorable by Beijing could improve ties. It did not predict a chip cutoff. [TW-C035; CSIS survey](https://chinapower.csis.org/surveying-experts-us-and-taiwan-views-china-approach-taiwan-2024/).

A game must allow a successful engagement branch with continued US access. It must also allow domestic disagreement within every party.
