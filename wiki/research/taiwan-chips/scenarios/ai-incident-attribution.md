# TW-X003: AI incident to chip crisis

Status: invented trigger with evidence-backed constraints. Window: 2026-2035.

A model-linked incident affects a chip business or a public system. Officials suspect a foreign actor. The incident could involve a rogue model, an authorized operator, a third party, an accident, or a false public report. This module does not assign probabilities to these causes.

No reviewed Taiwan or semiconductor source establishes that this event occurred. TW-C052 is a hypothesis.

## Required distinctions

Keep these fields separate:

- Where the model was developed.
- Who operated the system.
- What the system could access.
- What happened and what damage followed.
- What each actor believes.
- The evidence for state direction.
- The confidence of independent investigators.

A model's national origin alone is insufficient evidence of state responsibility. This is an analytic rule for the game.

## Decision sequence

First, a firm can preserve records and limit immediate damage. Next, investigators assess competing causes. Political leaders choose what to claim in public. Agencies then choose whether to restrict trade. Other actors choose whether to retaliate.

Do not merge these decisions into one automatic transition. TSMC governance is distinct from Taiwan's cabinet. A US regulator also has its own licensing role. [TW-C012; TSMC board](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/board-of-directors); [TW-C027; BIS, 2026-01-13](https://media.bis.gov/press-release/department-commerce-revises-license-review-policy-semiconductors-exported-china).

## Branches

An investigation can support de-escalation. A premature accusation can trigger export limits. A company can dispute an official account. New evidence can reverse an earlier finding. Reversible measures can then be lifted while some distrust remains.

These are original branches. The sources establish institutions and supply dependencies. They do not establish transition rates.

## Why Taiwan matters

A political response could affect new chip supply, contracts and investor confidence. Broad economic action can also impose costs on the responding states. [TW-C039; Rhodium and Atlantic Council](https://rhg.com/research/sanctioning-china-in-a-taiwan-crisis-scenarios-and-risks/).

Keep the investigation accessible to the player. Hidden attribution can be a game variable, but the narrator must distinguish evidence from belief. Do not present a guessed attacker as a historical fact.
