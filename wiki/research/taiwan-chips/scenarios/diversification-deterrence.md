# TW-X005: Diversification and deterrence

Status: hypothesis. Window: 2030-2035. Net effect unknown.

More production outside Taiwan can reduce disruption costs. It can also change what leaders think other states will defend. Those effects need not move in the same direction.

TSMC reports real Arizona production. It also has further investment plans. In July 2026, C.C. Wei declined to give a fixed schedule for the additional US investment. [TW-C010; TSMC report](https://investor.tsmc.com/static/annualReports/2025/english/index.html); [TW-C015; call transcript](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/encrypt/files/encrypt_file/reports/2026-08/3e494f0c14dd0890f897aa044415e21d93486cc4/TSMC%202Q26%20Transcript.pdf).

## Four branches

1. **More time for diplomacy.** Alternate production reduces panic after a disruption. Partners retain security interests in Taiwan.
2. **A mistaken signal of disengagement.** Beijing infers that lower chip dependence means lower US commitment. A crisis tests that belief.
3. **Plans miss demand.** Construction, qualification or input constraints leave dependence higher than leaders expected.
4. **Distributed but interdependent production.** More countries host fabs, but all still need foreign equipment, materials or expertise.

These branches are analysis. No reviewed study gives their relative probabilities.

## The silicon shield has limits

The CFR task force judged that Taiwan's chip role raises conflict costs but cannot guarantee protection. A strategic interest can survive after some chip production moves. Conversely, economic stakes alone may fail to deter force. [TW-C041; CFR, 2023](https://www.cfr.org/task-force-reports/us-taiwan-relations-in-a-new-era/cdn/ff/Ig_ZOuCbVzeNEasQrQ3Evyn2UdT3rn-2TreCp8i-BqE/1687531766/public/2023-06/TFR81_U.S.-TaiwanRelationsNewEra_SinglePages_2023-06-05_Online.pdf).

SIA's October 2026 blog still identifies imported upstream inputs as necessary to US production. More US fabs do not imply complete self-sufficiency. [TW-C047; SIA](https://www.semiconductors.org/sia-report-highlights-strategies-for-strengthening-supply-chains-supporting-the-u-s-chip-ecosystem/).

## Model guardrails

Keep planned spending, installed tools, qualified output, packaging and service access separate. Keep each process generation separate. ESMC's stated process mix is not a direct substitute for all frontier AI logic. [TW-C017; ESMC announcement](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3049).

The 2024 SIA/BCG estimate concerns aggregate fab capacity through 2032. It supplies no precise 2035 AI independence threshold. [TW-C031; publisher summary](https://www.semiconductors.org/policies/supply-chain-security/).
