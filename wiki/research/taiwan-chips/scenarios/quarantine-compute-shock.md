# TW-X002: Quarantine to AI hardware shock

Status: hypothesis. Window: 2026-2035. No probability assigned.

Beijing demands greater control over traffic linked to Taiwan. Some firms delay shipments. The game must distinguish delay from a complete trade stop.

CSIS defines a quarantine as law-enforcement-led control. It defines a blockade as a military-led campaign to cut traffic. Its quarantine study stresses the role of commercial compliance. These are analytic definitions. They do not settle the legality of a real action. [TW-C032–033; CSIS, 2024-06-05](https://features.csis.org/chinapower/china-quarantine-taiwan/).

## State variables

Track shipment delay, insurer confidence, order inventory, qualified alternate output, public attribution of responsibility, diplomatic contact and use of force. Track civilian harm separately.

New chip deliveries feed future compute growth. They are not the installed compute stock. Existing systems can continue unless a separate event affects power, parts, software or maintenance. This stock-flow distinction is analysis, not a numerical estimate. See TW-C050.

## Branches

| Choice or event | Possible result | Evidence status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carriers comply | Political pressure rises before all trade stops. | Mechanism from CSIS. |
| A temporary agreement is accepted | Traffic resumes while sovereignty disputes remain. | Original branch. |
| A disputed encounter causes injury | Governments widen action or pause it. | Original branch. |
| Inventories and qualified alternatives absorb demand | AI deployment slows without immediate loss of the installed fleet. | Analytic branch. |
| A blockade persists | Civilian hardship and escalation pressure rise. | Research warning. |

The available publisher summary of *Lights Out* warns that blockade is not a low-risk option. Its full assumptions were not reviewed here. [TW-C037; CSIS, 2025-07-31](https://www.csis.org/analysis/lights-out-wargaming-chinese-blockade-taiwan).

## Economic limit

Rhodium's exposure estimate assumes all Taiwan trade stops. Do not apply it to a short or selective quarantine. Do not label exposed activity as GDP lost. [TW-C038; Rhodium, 2022-12-14](https://rhg.com/research/taiwan-economic-disruptions/).

The shock can harm Chinese firms as well as US firms. Relative AI advantage depends on stocks, dependence and recovery. It is not determined by the location of the first disruption.

Useful restraint choices include verified pauses, clear notices, continued talks and limited reversible measures. These are game branches, not claims that actors will choose them.
