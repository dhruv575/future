# TW-X004: Controls to bargaining crisis

Status: hypothesis. Window: 2026-2035.

A government changes access to AI chips or chipmaking tools during a Taiwan dispute. The first choice concerns the scope of a rule. The next choices concern licenses, compliance and response.

Controls are not fixed. The December 2024 BIS package expanded tool, software and HBM restrictions. In January 2026, BIS announced conditional case-by-case review for H200, MI325X and similar chips. [TW-C026; BIS 2024](https://www.bis.gov/press-release/commerce-strengthens-export-controls-restrict-chinas-capability-produce-advanced-semiconductors-military); [TW-C027; BIS 2026](https://media.bis.gov/press-release/department-commerce-revises-license-review-policy-semiconductors-exported-china).

## Model each rule

Record the controlled item, end user, destination, re-export scope, effective date, review policy, exceptions and expiration. Record a license outcome separately from a rule announcement.

The Netherlands has its own authorization requirements. An allied government can agree on a security objective yet disagree on scope or timing. [TW-C028; Dutch government](https://www.government.nl/latest/news/2025/01/15/klever-export-controls-on-advanced-semiconductor-manufacturing-equipment-to-be-tightened).

## Competing branches

| Branch | Possible effect |
| --- | --- |
| Partners coordinate narrow controls | Some access falls; coalition cohesion remains. |
| Conditional licenses permit trade | Supply resumes under monitored conditions. |
| Both sides broaden retaliation | Costs, uncertainty and coalition disputes rise. |
| Firms invest in replacements | Future control power declines if alternatives work. |
| A negotiated suspension takes effect | Some costs fall; verification remains disputed. |

These outcomes are conditional. CSIS argues that controls can accelerate replacement of US inputs. This is a competing mechanism, not proof that every control fails. [TW-C043; CSIS, 2024-10-04](https://www.csis.org/analysis/double-edged-sword-semiconductor-export-controls).

Rhodium and the Atlantic Council describe reasons for selective Chinese retaliation and coalition division. A symmetric tit-for-tat rule is therefore too simple. [TW-C040; report](https://rhg.com/research/retaliation-and-resilience-chinas-economic-statecraft-in-a-taiwan-crisis/).

## Restraint

A player can define measurable conditions for relief, protect civilian continuity, or maintain technical contact. These are design options. No source establishes their success rate.

Do not make economic confrontation automatically cause armed war. A separate political choice and trigger are required.
