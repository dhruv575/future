# Taiwan politics and legal gates

The game must represent voters, parties, executive office, the legislature and firms separately. They have different powers.

## Dated baseline

The official October 10, 2026 speech names Lai Ching-te, Hsiao Bi-khim, Cho Jung-tai and Han Kuo-yu in their respective offices. This supports a cutoff-date snapshot. It does not determine who holds office in 2028 or 2032. [TW-C005; official speech](https://english.president.gov.tw/News/7226).

The KMT's public engagement policy and the TPP's separate leadership require separate actor records. Do not code either party as a branch of the PRC state. There can be cooperation, disagreement and factional differences. [TW-C006; KMT indexed statement](https://www1.kmt.org.tw/english/page.aspx?anum=25939&mnum=112&type=article); [TW-C008; TPP leadership](https://www.tpp.org.tw/en/leaders-modal.php?mid=61).

This is not a claim that political influence is absent. It is a rule against treating a disputed political label as demonstrated control.

## Decision gates

| Action | Gate in the reviewed sources | Game implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elect president | Direct election; four-year term | Model election and office transfer separately. |
| Issue emergency decree | Executive Yuan resolution and legislative ratification | A crisis does not remove all institutional limits. |
| Make a covered political agreement with the mainland | Article 5-3 process | A negotiating leader cannot alone complete it. |
| Change constitution or territory | Supermajority and referendum process | Use the specific legal threshold. |
| Restrict a class of exports | Exact future statutory route not established here | Require a separate legal source. |
| Replace TSMC executives | Corporate board authority | Government control and corporate control are separate. |

Sources: [Constitution](https://english.president.gov.tw/Page/95), [Cross-Strait Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/ENG/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=Q0010001), [TSMC board](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/board-of-directors).

The table describes the reviewed texts. It is not a complete legal opinion. A future law, court ruling or unlawful act can change the available path. Record that event explicitly.

## Taiwan's public is a separate actor

Party choice, identity, desired constitutional status and willingness to bear costs are different variables. NCCU publishes a long-running trend series and explains how it combines surveys. This module has not extracted its latest chart values. Do not invent a public-opinion baseline. [TW-C046; NCCU](https://esc.nccu.edu.tw/PageDoc/Detail?fid=7801&id=6963).

## Beijing's stated position

The PRC retains force as an option. Its domestic law identifies conditions that it says can justify non-peaceful measures. These are PRC claims and rules. They do not establish Taiwan's consent or international legal acceptance. [TW-C044; law text](https://china.usc.edu/node/20381); [TW-C045; official white-paper summary](https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/whitepaper/202208/10/content_WS62f34f46c6d02e533532f0ac.html).

AI competition can change perceived costs and urgency. None of these sources makes AI a necessary cause of a Taiwan conflict.
