# Research gaps and update rules

The strongest unresolved issue is the link from a future political choice to a specific chip restriction. The corpus does not establish that link for any Taiwan party.

## Before a playable baseline

1. Check current Chinese-language Taiwan law and relevant court decisions. Establish the precise authority for selective export controls, emergency measures and investment restrictions.
2. Extract current legislative seat counts and coalition rules from official records. Recheck party leadership. Do not use the 2024 presidential vote as a 2026 legislature.
3. Read NCCU chart data and methods. Keep survey questions and respondent dates. Separate polling about identity, constitutional preference and party choice.
4. Build a product-specific capacity baseline. Include qualified output, packaging, memory, service, inventories and imports. Announced dollars are not a capacity unit.
5. Read the full *Lights Out* report. Record assumptions, scenario coverage and sensitivity. The current entry is abstract-level only.
6. Recheck export rules and actual license policy by item, destination and end user. The January 2026 BIS announcement is not the entire October 2026 rulebook.
7. Add primary evidence from Chinese firms and chip-input producers. This module has limited coverage of SMIC, Huawei, domestic tools, materials and replacement paths.
8. Add Taiwan chip firms beyond TSMC, including UMC, ASE, MediaTek and server makers. Treat them as a coverage queue until sources support profiles.
9. Add partners' legal and political constraints. Japan, South Korea, the Netherlands and the EU are independent decision actors.
10. Seek research that tests whether chip dependence changes deterrence. Current arguments establish plausible directions, not a calibrated net effect.

## Keep the source type visible

Company statements support stated plans. They do not prove completion. Official political statements support declared positions. They do not reveal all motives. Expert surveys describe selected respondents. Wargames test assumptions and mechanisms. Neither gives an automatic event probability.

The [source register](../../data/parts/taiwan-chips/sources.json) records blocked pages and partial access. Failed access is not hidden.

## Update triggers

Update after elections, leadership changes, court rulings, new controls, actual license changes, fab qualification, material capacity changes or a major crisis.

Keep the old claim and add a dated replacement. Record why the old baseline changed. Do not silently overwrite a forecast with its later outcome.

For an AI incident, store competing explanations and evidence quality. Distinguish attribution confidence from political confidence. A persuasive statement is not necessarily a verified finding.
