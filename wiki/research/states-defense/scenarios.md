# Escalation and restraint branches

Horizon: 2026-2035. All triggers below are hypotheses. Sources support the component mechanisms. They do not predict the whole chain. No branch has a calibrated probability.

## SD-X001: Autonomous maritime incident becomes an alliance crisis

Trigger: An autonomous system causes harm in a disputed maritime encounter.

- **Preserve evidence and hold political consultation.** Next: Incident investigation. Allows accident, misuse and state-order explanations to remain distinct.
- **Attribute the event before evidence is strong.** Next: Alliance confrontation. Public commitments can narrow diplomatic room.
- **Agree a pause and incident review.** Next: Managed competition. Risk falls if the pause is implemented.

Limits: The trigger is invented. No source says this specific event will occur. Treaty trigger and response are separate decisions.

Sources: [U.S. Department of Defense: U.S.-Philippines Bilateral Defense Guidelines Fact Sheet](https://media.defense.gov/2023/May/03/2003214451/-1/-1/0/BILATERAL-DEFENSE-GUIDELINES-FACT-SHEET.PDF), [U.S. Department of Defense: DoD Directive 3000.09: Autonomy in Weapon Systems](https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodd/300009p.pdf), [UK Ministry of Defence: AUKUS statement: 26 September 2024](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/aukus-statement-26-september-2024).

## SD-X002: AI advice converts uncertainty into nuclear alarm

Trigger: During a conventional crisis, an AI tool interprets ambiguous warning as hostile nuclear intent.

- **Require independent assessment and confirm channels.** Next: Uncertainty under review. Creates a chance to correct a false alarm.
- **Trust a confident output despite weak evidence.** Next: Nuclear crisis. Human decision makers increase alert or issue threats.
- **Reaffirm limits and maintain dialogue.** Next: Contained conventional crisis. Reduces concern about nuclear intent.

Limits: Human control can coexist with poor human advice. No automatic model-to-launch transition. No operational launch procedure is modeled.

Sources: [Congressional Research Service: Authority to Launch Nuclear Forces](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10521), [Congressional Research Service: Defense Primer: Nuclear Command, Control, and Communications (NC3)](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF11697), [SIPRI: AI in Chinese, Indian and US Nuclear Postures, Norms and Systems](https://www.sipri.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/2602_ai_integration.pdf), [RAND: How Might Artificial Intelligence Affect the Risk of Nuclear War?](https://www.rand.org/pubs/perspectives/PE296.html), [Carnegie Endowment for International Peace: Why Is Nuclear Entanglement So Dangerous?](https://carnegieendowment.org/europe/posts/2019/01/why-is-nuclear-entanglement-so-dangerous), [PRC State Council / Xinhua: Xi, Biden affirm need to enhance international AI cooperation](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202411/17/content_WS67394d2ac6d0868f4e8ed13c.html).

## SD-X003: Taiwan political change becomes a supply and security dispute

Trigger: A future Taiwan government seeks closer PRC ties and proposes a restrictive technology policy.

- **Use domestic review and commercial negotiation.** Next: Political and trade dispute. Supply restrictions may be amended or rejected.
- **US treats the proposal as an immediate security loss.** Next: Coercive diplomacy. Economic and military signals may interact.
- **Agree narrow safeguards and trade continuity.** Next: Managed adjustment. Political change can occur without war.

Limits: A party label is not a policy prediction. TSMC control, export permission and technical supply continuity require separate company research. An election alone does not transfer sovereignty or mandate a chip cutoff.

Sources: [Congressional Research Service: Taiwan: Background and U.S. Relations](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10275), [Congressional Research Service: Taiwan: Defense and Military Issues](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF12481), [Government of the Netherlands: The Netherlands expands export control measure for advanced semiconductor manufacturing equipment](https://www.government.nl/latest/news/2024/09/06/the-netherlands-expands-export-control-measure-advanced-semiconductor-manufacturing-equipment).

## SD-X004: Rogue-model cyber event is mistaken for state action

Trigger: A model-assisted or model-directed incident crosses borders while political tension is high.

- **Separate technical attribution from authorization.** Next: Joint or parallel investigation. Keeps accident and unauthorized use plausible.
- **Assume the model owner proves state intent.** Next: Retaliation pressure. False attribution can intensify the crisis.
- **Contain the affected system and exchange evidence.** Next: Incident control. May enable a reciprocal pause.

Limits: The rogue-model trigger is speculative. Volt Typhoon sources do not establish rogue AI. No technical attack methods are included.

Sources: [SIPRI: AI in Chinese, Indian and US Nuclear Postures, Norms and Systems](https://www.sipri.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/2602_ai_integration.pdf), [CISA and partners: Joint Fact Sheet for Leaders on PRC-sponsored Volt Typhoon Cyber Activity](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCISA/bulletins/39171e0), [PRC Ministry of Foreign Affairs: PRC response on US-China cybersecurity dialogue](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/eng/wjb/zzjg_663340/jks_665232/jkxw_665234/202504/t20250414_11594102.html).

## SD-X005: Conventional crisis threatens confidence in shared warning systems

Trigger: A crisis disrupts a service used for both conventional operations and nuclear warning.

- **Explain limited intent through an established channel.** Next: Verification and restraint. Can reduce fear if the explanation is credible.
- **Infer preparation for a disarming attack.** Next: Nuclear signaling crisis. Pressure rises despite uncertainty about intent.
- **Restore service and negotiate mutual limits.** Next: Reduced entanglement risk. Creates an exit if both sides accept the limits.

Limits: This is a general causal scenario. No specific system vulnerability or target is identified. A nuclear outcome is possible in the hypothesis, not inevitable.
