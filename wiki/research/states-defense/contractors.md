# Defense firms and corporate choices

Research cutoff: 2026-10-10. These profiles describe selected suppliers. Product claims are attributed. No effectiveness score is assigned.

## Palantir: SD-E066 and SD-E067

Palantir offers defense data and AI software. A May 2026 company release names Alex Karp as co-founder and CEO. The product and release text were available as official search extracts. The direct pages returned little body text. [Defense offering](https://www.palantir.com/offerings/defense), [company release, 2026-05-04](https://investors.palantir.com/news-details/2026/Palantir-Reports-Q1-2026-U-S--Revenue-Growth-of-104-YY-and-Revenue-Growth-of-85/).

Game choices, as analysis: product access, contract terms, support, testing and public advocacy. A corporate executive has influence over supply. The military chain retains its own authority. [CRS command structure](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10542).

## Anduril: SD-E068 to SD-E070

Anduril describes Lattice as software for defense and public-safety tasks. Its September 2026 partnership announcement identifies Palmer Luckey as founder. A January 2025 company statement identifies Brian Schimpf as CEO. That older statement does not verify his October 2026 tenure. These company pages were read through indexed extracts; direct body access failed. [Lattice](https://www.anduril.com/news/anduril-s-lattice-a-trusted-dual-use-commercial-and-military-platform-for-public-safety-security), [Luckey and partnership, 2026-09-29](https://www.anduril.com/news/anduril-and-voyager-establish-strategic-partnership-across-advanced-weapons-and-propulsion), [leadership statement, 2025-01-23](https://www.anduril.com/news/anduril-announces-key-leadership-promotions).

Keep founder, chief executive, product and customer separate. Production commitments, delivery, tests and authorized use require different state fields. This is model analysis. A partnership announcement does not establish delivery.

## Lockheed Martin: SD-E071 and SD-E072

The live company biography identifies Jim Taiclet as chairman, president and CEO. In company-hosted 2023 testimony, he supported defense integration of AI and other digital technologies for deterrence. The direct pages failed; official search extracts support these narrow claims. [Biography](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/who-we-are/leadership-governance/james-taiclet.html), [2023 testimony](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/news/statements-speeches/2023/hasc-statement---jim-taiclet.html).

Game choices, as analysis: investment, integration partnerships and delivery commitments. Do not grant this supplier the military customer's use-of-force authority.

## RTX: SD-E073 and SD-E074

RTX's live biography names Christopher Calio as chairman and CEO. It identifies Collins Aerospace, Pratt & Whitney and Raytheon as the group's businesses. The page is undated; it is an access-date snapshot. [RTX biography](https://www.rtx.com/who-we-are/corporate-governance/christopher-calio).

RTX reported AI-assisted engine inspection in July 2026. It describes support to human inspectors and applications to commercial and military engines. This is evidence of an AI support product. It does not establish autonomous lethal decision-making. Performance claims remain company claims. [RTX release, 2026-07-15](https://www.rtx.com/news/news-center/2026/07/15/rtxs-pratt-whitney-advances-engine-inspections-with-ai-powered-technology).

## AVIC: SD-E075

The January 2024 BluePath/CASI study identifies AVIC as a state-owned aviation group. It documents military aircraft and unmanned systems. This historical study supports the industrial actor. It does not establish current leadership or unrestricted autonomous weapon use. [AVIC organization study](https://bluepathlabs.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/organization-and-structure-of-the-aviation-industry-corporation-of-china-avic-jan-2024.pdf).

Game choices, as analysis: industrial investment, production and research priorities. Model state ownership, company governance and PLA command as separate relations. Do not turn a group with many subsidiaries into one unrestricted military decision-maker.
