# Nuclear forces and human control

Research cutoff: 2026-10-10. Inventory snapshot: January 2026. These are estimates, not inspection results.

## Comparable inventory categories

SIPRI separates deployed warheads, military stockpiles and total inventories. A stockpile includes deployed and stored warheads available for military use. A total inventory also includes retired warheads awaiting dismantlement. Deployed warheads are on missiles or at operational bases. These categories do not mean identical readiness. [SIPRI Yearbook 2026 summary, printed page 11](https://www.sipri.org/sites/default/files/2026-06/yb26_summary_en_1.pdf).

| State | Deployed | Military stockpile | Total inventory | Entity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| United States | 1,770 | 3,700 | 5,042 | SD-E049 |
| Russia | 1,796 | 4,400 | 5,420 | SD-E051 |
| China | 34 | 620 | 620 | SD-E050 |
| United Kingdom | 120 | 225 | 225 | SD-E052 |
| France | 280 | 290 | 370 | SD-E054 |
| North Korea | Nil or a negligible value | 60 | 60 | SD-E053 |

All values are approximate. The table does not state how many weapons could be used in a specific crisis. Do not add deployed warheads to the stockpile column. They are already included. Do not compare these values with treaty-accountable totals without checking definitions. [SIPRI table and notes](https://www.sipri.org/sites/default/files/2026-06/yb26_summary_en_1.pdf).

China's estimated stockpile increased to 620. SIPRI also gives a conditional assessment of possible ICBM parity with the US or Russia around 2030. ICBMs are missiles. That assessment is not warhead parity, a war forecast, or proof of intent to attack. [SIPRI release, 2026-06-08](https://www.sipri.org/media/press-release/2026/increasing-focus-nuclear-weapons-amid-heightened-escalation-risks-new-sipri-yearbook-out-now).

## US authority and systems

The US President can authorize nuclear use. Military and civilian advisers support the decision. They do not have an independent authority to authorize use. The command system must support lawful orders and required human action. This is an authority map, not a launch procedure. [CRS authority report, 2025-12-11](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10521), [CRS NC3 report, 2026-01-08](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF11697).

The US triad has land-based intercontinental missiles, submarine-launched missiles and bombers. The cited December 2025 overview distinguishes current Minuteman III and Ohio-class systems from planned Sentinel and Columbia replacements. Modernization schedules and costs can change. A funded or announced replacement is not a fielded capability. [CRS strategic nuclear forces, 2025-12-16](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10519).

That CRS overview predates the February 2026 expiration of New START. Use SIPRI's June 2026 account for that later treaty status. Do not carry the older treaty date forward. [SIPRI release, 2026-06-08](https://www.sipri.org/media/press-release/2026/increasing-focus-nuclear-weapons-amid-heightened-escalation-risks-new-sipri-yearbook-out-now).

## PRC doctrine and evidence limits

The PRC's November 2025 white paper declares no first use. It describes a limited deterrent and centralized nuclear command under the Central Military Commission. It also rejects an AI arms race. These are public policy declarations. They are not independent verification of force readiness, internal plans or compliance. [PRC white paper, 2025-11-27](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/eng/zy/wjzc/202511/t20251127_11761656.html).

The same source supports a role for Xi through the CMC institution. It does not justify copying the US sole-authorization model into the PRC game actor. Public evidence about internal PRC nuclear deliberation is limited. Keep the authority fields separate. [PRC white paper](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/eng/zy/wjzc/202511/t20251127_11761656.html), [SIPRI AI study, February 2026](https://www.sipri.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/2602_ai_integration.pdf).

## AI and control

Xi and Biden affirmed human control over nuclear-use decisions in November 2024. This was a political commitment. It was not a verified system design or an inspection regime. US law includes a policy statement that AI should not compromise nuclear safeguards or required human actions. [Official PRC meeting account, 2024-11-17](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202411/17/content_WS67394d2ac6d0868f4e8ed13c.html), [CRS NC3 report](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF11697).

The risk can occur upstream. AI can change what an official sees, how confident the official feels, and what the official thinks an opponent can do. Human approval can coexist with flawed advice. RAND discusses possible instability and possible benefits. Its source horizon is 2040. SIPRI studies public evidence and norms in 2026. Neither proves that an LLM now controls nuclear launch. [RAND, 2018](https://www.rand.org/pubs/perspectives/PE296.html), [SIPRI, 2026](https://www.sipri.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/2602_ai_integration.pdf).

## Entanglement and restraint

Some services support both conventional missions and nuclear warning or command. A conventional crisis can therefore create fear about nuclear survival. Acton's analysis describes this escalation mechanism. It does not supply a calibrated probability. [Acton, 2019](https://carnegieendowment.org/europe/posts/2019/01/why-is-nuclear-entanglement-so-dangerous), [CRS NC3 report](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF11697).

Proposed game restraints: independent review of ambiguous evidence; communication of limited intent; maintained political channels; recovery of disrupted services; and negotiated limits. These are analysis-based choices. Each can fail if evidence, trust or implementation is weak. They should remain available before a terminal war state. See [scenario SD-X002 and SD-X005](scenarios.md).

Russia, North Korea, France and the UK retain separate decision processes. Their arsenal sizes do not justify automatic entry into a US-China conflict. This is a modeling limit. The corpus does not reconstruct their nuclear command procedures.
