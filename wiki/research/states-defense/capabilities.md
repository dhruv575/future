# Defense capabilities and AI

Research cutoff: 2026-10-10. Capability, readiness, intent and authority are separate fields.

## Military AI and LLMs: SD-E056

AI can support analysis and decisions. An LLM is one type of AI model. It is not the same as a weapon, a sensor, or a command authority. Public nuclear-control research distinguishes human control of nuclear use from broader human judgment in conventional systems. Keep those concepts separate in the game. [SIPRI AI study, February 2026](https://www.sipri.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/2602_ai_integration.pdf).

Proposed model roles: information processing, advice, simulation, logistics support and delegated task execution. Record which role is enabled in each scenario. A model's access to a tool is different from political authority to use force. The game must specify both. This is design analysis, not a claim that each state has each deployment.

## Autonomous weapon systems: SD-E055

The 2023 US directive requires appropriate human judgment over use of force. It establishes policy and review requirements. It does not prohibit every autonomous weapon. The official search extract was read; direct access was blocked. [DoD Directive 3000.09, 2023-01-25](https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodd/300009p.pdf).

Proposed fields: task scope, operator authority, test evidence, operating restrictions, and incident status. Do not infer autonomy from the absence of a pilot. Do not infer general intelligence from a limited autonomous task. These are classification rules for the corpus.

## Replicator and AUKUS: SD-E064 and SD-E065

DIU announced software vendors for Replicator in November 2024. The announcement supports a procurement step. It does not establish delivery of every announced system or all program goals. Direct access was blocked; the official search extract was read. [DIU announcement, 2024-11-20](https://www.diu.mil/latest/defense-innovation-unit-announces-software-vendors-to-support-replicator).

The AUKUS partners reported common AI algorithms on maritime patrol aircraft in September 2024. This is a concrete official integration claim. It is not independent evidence of combat performance. A 2026 ministerial page records continued cooperation; its linked statement was not fully read. [AUKUS statement, 2024-09-26](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/aukus-statement-26-september-2024), [2026 statement landing page](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/aukus-defence-ministerial-joint-statement-30-may-2026).

## Cyber capability: SD-E057

US and partner agencies attribute Volt Typhoon activity to PRC state sponsorship. They describe preparation for possible disruption of critical infrastructure. PRC officials reject that attribution. The accusation and denial are separately recorded. The denial does not by itself establish an alternative technical cause. [CISA notice, 2024-03-19](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCISA/bulletins/39171e0), [PRC response, 2025-04-14](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/eng/wjb/zzjg_663340/jks_665232/jkxw_665234/202504/t20250414_11594102.html).

These sources do not establish rogue AI. For a model-related incident, track technical cause, evidence confidence, operator control and state authorization separately. A model's origin is insufficient to establish a state's intent. These are analysis rules for [SD-X004](scenarios.md).

## Space and counterspace: SD-E059

Secure World Foundation's 2026 report covers several counterspace classes, including physical and non-physical means. The reviewed summary describes a varied and uncertain capability picture. The full country chapters were not read. Do not turn the summary into a precise readiness score. [SWF report page, 2026-04-08](https://www.swfound.org/publications-and-reports/2026-global-counterspace-capabilities-report).

Space services can support warning, communication and other missions. Loss of service can create uncertainty beyond the immediate event. Shared conventional and nuclear use can increase escalation risk. The corpus models service reliability and perceived intent. It does not identify targets or attack methods. [CRS NC3 report, 2026-01-08](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF11697), [Acton, 2019](https://carnegieendowment.org/europe/posts/2019/01/why-is-nuclear-entanglement-so-dangerous).

## Warning and command: SD-E058

NC3 supports warning, decision-making and authorized communication. AI can change the information that reaches people even when people retain the use decision. A confident output is not equivalent to correct evidence. The latter statement is a model rule supported by the decision-risk analysis. [CRS NC3 report](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF11697), [SIPRI AI study](https://www.sipri.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/2602_ai_integration.pdf).

The PLA formed its Information Support Force in April 2024 under direct CMC leadership. The announcement canceled the previous Strategic Support Force designation. Keep historical and current institutional names separate. The announcement does not disclose every internal command relationship. [PRC announcement, 2024-04-19](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202404/19/content_WS66225707c6d0868f4e8e63bd.html).

## Missile defense and Golden Dome: SD-E060

CRS describes Golden Dome as a proposed architecture with feasibility, cost and strategic questions. Defensive benefits and concern about arms-race effects are separate policy arguments. The cited December 2025 report does not establish a completed shield. Do not assign invulnerability. [CRS Golden Dome report, 2025-12-11](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IN12568).

## US, PRC and Taiwan conventional forces: SD-E061 to SD-E063

The 2026 US strategy emphasizes denial-based deterrence and greater allied contributions. It also calls for PLA engagement to support stability and de-escalation. Stated deterrence and diplomatic goals can coexist. Neither guarantees future conduct. [US National Defense Strategy, 2026-01-23](https://www.army.mil/e2/downloads/rv7/about/2026-NATIONAL-DEFENSE-STRATEGY.pdf).

The US 2025 China military report describes broad PLA modernization across nuclear, conventional, cyber and space domains. Direct access failed. Only official search excerpts support this module's broad categories. No exact current force count or readiness score is derived from it. [2025 report](https://media.defense.gov/2025/Dec/23/2003849070/-1/-1/1/ANNUALREPORT-TO-CONGRESS-MILITARY-AND-SECURITY-DEVELOPMENTS-INVOLVING-THEPEOPLES-REPUBLIC-OF-CHINA-2025.PDF).

Taiwan's defense choices depend on procurement, training, resources and external support. An announced sale is different from delivered equipment. US intervention remains a political choice. [CRS Taiwan defense, 2026-02-09](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF12481), [CRS Taiwan relations, 2025-08-15](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10275).

The CSIS invasion study tests a conventional 2026 campaign. The blockade study tests a different problem. Use them for separate scenario families. Their assumptions about resistance, intervention and access must remain visible. Neither gives a general estimate of AI-caused war through 2035. [CSIS invasion report](https://csis-website-prod.s3.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/publication/230109_Cancian_FirstBattle_NextWar.pdf), [CSIS blockade discussion](https://www.csis.org/analysis/lights-out-wargaming-blockade-taiwan).
