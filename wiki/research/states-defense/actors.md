# Governments and institutions

Research cutoff: 2026-10-10. These profiles separate a state, an institution and its office holder. A government is not one person. Authority descriptions follow public sources. Incentives and practical game limits are design analysis unless described as stated policy.

## SD-E001: United States government

Federal actor. Executive and legislature have separate powers.

Roles: Security policy; Defense; Diplomacy. Authority: Civilian direction of military operations.

Limits: Congressional funding and oversight; Allied consent is separate. Incentives: Protect security and continued trade; stated policy in 2026 strategy.

Sources: [Congressional Research Service: Defense Primer: Commanding U.S. Military Operations](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10542), [Congressional Research Service: Taiwan: Background and U.S. Relations](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10275), [U.S. Department of Defense: 2026 National Defense Strategy](https://www.army.mil/e2/downloads/rv7/about/2026-NATIONAL-DEFENSE-STRATEGY.pdf).

## SD-E002: People's Republic of China government

Party-led state. Its Taiwan sovereignty claim is disputed.

Roles: Security policy; Defense; Diplomacy. Authority: CMC directs armed forces.

Limits: Foreign actors retain their own choices; Public doctrine does not reveal all decisions. Incentives: Unification and national security; public PRC positions.

Sources: [PRC Ministry of Foreign Affairs: China's Arms Control, Disarmament, and Nonproliferation in the New Era](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/eng/zy/wjzc/202511/t20251127_11761656.html), [Congressional Research Service: Taiwan: Background and U.S. Relations](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10275), [PRC State Council / Xinhua: Xi presents flag to PLA's information support force](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202404/19/content_WS66225707c6d0868f4e8e63bd.html).

## SD-E003: Taiwan government (Republic of China)

Self-governing democracy. PRC claims sovereignty but has never controlled Taiwan.

Roles: Self-government; Defense; Trade policy. Authority: Domestic institutions decide public policy.

Limits: US defense intervention is not guaranteed. Incentives: Maintain public security and political choice; design analysis.

Sources: [Congressional Research Service: Taiwan: Background and U.S. Relations](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10275), [Congressional Research Service: Taiwan: Defense and Military Issues](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF12481).

## SD-E004: Japan government

US treaty ally with separate constitutional decisions.

Roles: Regional security; Alliance consultation. Authority: National policy and consent within treaty framework.

Limits: Treaty geographic scope; Constitutional processes. Incentives: National security and regional stability; design analysis.

Sources: [Prime Minister's Office of Japan: TAKAICHI Sanae: Prime Minister](https://japan.kantei.go.jp/105/meibo/daijin/takaichi_sanae.html), [Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan: Japan-U.S. Security Treaty](https://www.mofa.go.jp/na/st/page1we_000093.html).

## SD-E005: Philippines government

US treaty ally. Maritime incidents can create alliance crises.

Roles: Maritime security; Alliance consultation. Authority: National decisions under mutual defense framework.

Limits: An incident must be assessed; response is not automatic. Incentives: Protect sovereignty and avoid war; design analysis.

Sources: [Philippine Presidential Communications Office: Presidential Communications Office: October 2026 releases](https://pco.gov.ph/page/3/), [U.S. Department of Defense: U.S.-Philippines Bilateral Defense Guidelines Fact Sheet](https://media.defense.gov/2023/May/03/2003214451/-1/-1/0/BILATERAL-DEFENSE-GUIDELINES-FACT-SHEET.PDF).
