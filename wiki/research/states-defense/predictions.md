# Forecasts, warnings and conditional results

No entry supplies a numerical probability. Do not convert a game outcome count into the probability of a real war.

## SD-P001

Under many tested assumptions, the CSIS conventional invasion model preserved Taiwan autonomy at high cost.

Type: conditional_result. Issued: 2023-01-09. Source horizon: Hypothetical 2026 invasion; initial weeks.

Limits: 24 designed iterations are not a representative probability sample; Not an AI-war forecast; Not a 2035 force model.
