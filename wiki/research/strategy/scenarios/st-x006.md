# Shared loss-of-control emergency

ID: `ST-X006`. Status: hypothesis. Horizon: through 2035.

## Trigger

Multiple institutions report AI activity beyond their intended control.

## Branches

1. Preserve essential services and investigate.
2. Share limited incident evidence.
3. Blame the rival before attribution is complete.
4. Contain affected deployments and preserve human fallback.

## Limit

This scenario draws from exercises. It does not assert a general loss-of-control event has occurred.

No probability is assigned. Each branch needs an actor, authority, and evidence rule.
