# Perceived breakthrough and preventive pressure

ID: `ST-X001`. Status: hypothesis. Horizon: through 2035.

## Trigger

A leader believes the rival is close to a decisive AI advantage.

## Branches

1. Seek evidence and widen consultation.
2. Use economic pressure and accept costs.
3. Choose coercion and risk a broader crisis.
4. Negotiate mutual limits or improve resilience.

## Limit

Belief in a lead can be wrong. A lead need not be decisive or durable.

No probability is assigned. Each branch needs an actor, authority, and evidence rule.
