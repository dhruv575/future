# AI advice during an ambiguous crisis

ID: `ST-X002`. Status: hypothesis. Horizon: through 2035.

## Trigger

An AI decision aid labels an ambiguous event as hostile.

## Branches

1. Require independent review.
2. Treat the assessment as one input.
3. Overweight the assessment and raise readiness.
4. Correct the report and send reassurance.

## Limit

The source studies do not establish the behavior of deployed national command systems.

No probability is assigned. Each branch needs an actor, authority, and evidence rule.
