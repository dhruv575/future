# Compute growth meets a power constraint

ID: `ST-X004`. Status: hypothesis. Horizon: through 2035.

## Trigger

A planned AI expansion lacks timely power or grid access.

## Branches

1. Delay expansion.
2. Improve efficiency.
3. Buy or relocate lawful capacity.
4. Lobby for faster infrastructure development.

## Limit

A delay need not cause conflict. Demand projections are not fixed physical limits.

No probability is assigned. Each branch needs an actor, authority, and evidence rule.
