# AI and nuclear entanglement

ID: `ST-X003`. Status: hypothesis. Horizon: through 2035.

## Trigger

A disruption affects a function used in both conventional and nuclear systems.

## Branches

1. Restore communication and explain scope.
2. Interpret the event as strategic preparation.
3. Hold action while checking the cause.

## Limit

Intent, actual damage, and perceived threat must stay separate.

No probability is assigned. Each branch needs an actor, authority, and evidence rule.

## Evidence

- [Averting AI Armageddon](https://www.cnas.org/publications/reports/averting-ai-armageddon).
- [Escalation Through Entanglement](https://carnegieendowment.org/china/posts/2018/12/escalation-through-entanglement-how-the-vulnerability-of-command-and-control-systems-raises-the-risks-of-an-inadvertent-nuclear-war).
