# Rivals choose verified restraint

ID: `ST-X005`. Status: hypothesis. Horizon: through 2035.

## Trigger

Both states seek lower shared AI risk while protecting secrets.

## Branches

1. Build domestic oversight.
2. Exchange narrow safety evidence.
3. Reject intrusive verification.
4. Continue competition with incident channels.

## Limit

Mutual interest does not imply a treaty or compliance.

No probability is assigned. Each branch needs an actor, authority, and evidence rule.
