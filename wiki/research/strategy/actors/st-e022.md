# AI strategic decision support

ID: `ST-E022`. Evidence date: 2023-03.

Systems can assist information review and the generation of options.

## Role and limits

- Capability class. Authority stays with the deploying institution.
- Testing does not prove reliable judgment about an adversary’s intent.

## Game use

Model assumptions depend on the organization or operator.

Treat the listed options as design assumptions. Do not infer private beliefs.

## Evidence

- [Reducing the Risks of Artificial Intelligence for Military Decision Advantage](https://cset.georgetown.edu/publication/reducing-the-risks-of-artificial-intelligence-for-military-decision-advantage/).
