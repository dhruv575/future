# Restraint and cooperation

Cooperation needs actors, authority, and credible evidence.
Do not represent it as a single trust score.

| Choice | Potential benefit | Cost or limit | Evidence basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Keep humans responsible for high-stakes decisions. | Preserves a point for review and refusal. | Humans can still accept bad advice under pressure. | [CNAS summary](https://www.cnas.org/publications/reports/averting-ai-armageddon) |
| Build domestic oversight. | Makes commitments more verifiable. | Firms and states may resist disclosure. | [RAND formal-model summary](https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA5163-1.html) |
| Exchange narrow safety information. | Helps each side reduce common risks. | Some tests can also reveal capability information. | [Sheehan's proposal](https://carnegieendowment.org/emissary/2026/07/ai-safety-parallel-us-china) |
| Preserve human fallback and essential services. | Limits harm if AI systems fail. | Maintaining fallback systems costs money and staff time. | [RAND exercise summary](https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA4767-3.html) |
| Improve infrastructure resilience. | Reduces the perceived value of coercion. | It can be slow and expensive. | [AGI Rideout summary](https://www.rand.org/pubs/perspectives/PEA4347-1.html) |
| Prepare channels before agreement is possible. | Keeps future options open. | Political conditions may still prevent use. | [RAND contingency summary](https://www.rand.org/pubs/perspectives/PEA4825-1.html) |

Benefits in this table are proposed mechanisms.
They are not guaranteed effects.

## Game design

A hotline needs a contact, a mandate, and a response.
Record whether it exists, whether it works, and whether the other party answers.
These can have different values.

A pause needs a scope and an end condition.
Record whether it covers one deployment, one training run, or a broader program.
Record who can check it and what information they can see.

A shared investigation needs protected evidence exchange.
It does not require unlimited access to a rival's systems.
Each side can accept technical containment while disputing political responsibility.

## Peaceful endings

Possible endings include restored trade, verified containment, limited agreements, and stable competition.
A costly compromise can still reduce harm.
A public agreement can fail during implementation.
Keep these distinctions in the outcome record.
